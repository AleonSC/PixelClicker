# PixelTitleWanderers

Moved from CLAUDE.md. The script header comments and `CLAUDE.md` index point here.

## PixelTitleWanderers.cs

**Vendors wandering the main menu** (component added to the title screen in `PixelTitleScreen.Build`, layer under `foreground`, behind the bar / logo / buttons): a small tick-box toggle **at the bottom left that is invisible until the mouse is over it** (a third, **'Blur background'** (**while it is ticked its text label is replaced by a blur-strength slider**: `PixelTitleWanderers.BlurAmount`, global PlayerPrefs `PixelClicker.Setting.TitleBlurAmount`, 1 = full blur, read by `PixelTitleScreen.BlurLevelsForAmount`) (the **'Display floor'** tick box is no longer on the menu: it is in Settings > Display right under 'Floor style', same global pref; the menu's **'Pets Wander'** toggle sits directly above the vendors toggle; **the same NPC never walks twice at once**, `Walker.key`) (`PixelTitleWanderers.BlurBackground`, PlayerPrefs `PixelClicker.Setting.TitleBlurBackground`, default ON), stacked above the live one: OFF = no blur - with the live scene the blur layers are simply hidden and the real scene shows sharp behind the title, without it the floor picture is blitted into a full-resolution RT (`PixelTitleScreen.UpdateSharp`); the wandering vendors and the toggles **fade out the moment Play is pressed** (`PixelTitleWanderers.FadeOut`, whatever the settings); a fourth, **'Display floor'** (`PixelTitleWanderers.DisplayFloor`, PlayerPrefs `PixelClicker.Setting.DisplayFloor`, default OFF (the floor is invisible unless ticked), global): OFF = `PixelFloor` never draws the floor in the game (`FloorOff`: visibility target 0, no flat base, props stay; the renderer is only `forceRenderingOff`, so the collider still catches pixels) and only the sky shows; a second one stacked above it, **'Live background'** (`PixelTitleWanderers.LiveBackground`, PlayerPrefs `PixelClicker.Setting.TitleLiveBackground`, default ON = the live blurred game scene behind the title as before; OFF = no camera render: `PixelTitleScreen.UpdateBlur` blits the floor style's picture (`PixelFloor.TryGetTitlePicture`: the active - animated - texture, tiled 6 across for repeating styles, 1x for fitted / stretched ones, a flat tint for Classic) into the same low-res live RT, so the usual blur chain turns it into a blurred full-screen floor behind the black bar); both appear while the mouse is over either) (`PixelTitleWanderers.Enabled`, PlayerPrefs `PixelClicker.Setting.TitleWanderers`, global, default off). While on, every `arriveEvery` seconds (max `maxAtOnce` at once) a vendor walks in from the left or right edge along the bottom of the screen (feet `feetHeight` up, `walkerHeight` 230 tall, `walkSpeed`), makes `stops` stops at random spots (standing `pauseRange` s, sometimes turning to look round, walking bob / sway, mirrored to face his direction) and walks off the nearer-random side; with `stayChance` (25%) a vendor 'stays for a while' (3x the stops, 2x the pauses). Cast = Cubie (`PixelGuideVendor.BuildWalker`, which preserves the visit's own pupil / arm / gift references) and every `PixelVisitorMinigame` (`BuildWalker`: a holder + 'Art' child drawn by the vendor's own `DrawVisitor`); the Egg and Bean cats too (whether or not they were met). All of them vanish when the title screen ends. Unscaled time.

**Pets & UFO (added later):** a fifth stacked toggle **'Pets'** (`PixelTitleWanderers.PetsEnabled`, PlayerPrefs `PixelClicker.Setting.TitlePets`, default off, global) shows the CURRENT save's pets hopping along the bottom of the menu (`UpdatePets`: a random `maxPets` (6) of `PixelPets.TitlePets()`, rebuilt whenever the owned list / outfits change; each pet is a hidden studio - `PixelPets.BuildTitleModel` = the real-look pixel + its outfit under a pivot, an orthographic camera into a transparent RenderTexture shown by a RawImage, re-rendered every frame - hopping with squash and stretch every `petRest` s) The UFO is NOT behind that toggle: if the UFO Helper is bought in the save and not switched off in its own Toggles tab switch (`PixelRobotWorker.Active`), a block-drawn flying saucer drifts along the top (`UpdateUfo`, `BuildUfoArt`). **The UFO abducts**: every `abductEvery` (0 = 14-32) s, if a vendor is walking or a pet is hopping on the ground (on screen), it flies over one (`UpdateAbduction`: `Ab.Fly` at 1.8x speed, `Ab.Beam`), switches on a translucent green beam (`BuildBeam` / `UpdateBeam`, a child of the saucer) and pulls the victim up over `abductSeconds` (0 = 2.4) while it shrinks and spins (`Walker.abducted` / `Hopper.abducted` make `Step` / `StepHopper` skip it); it is then removed (`FinishAbduction`; abducted pets come back after 20 s via `petRespawnAt`, vendors just keep arriving). `disableAbduction` turns it off; clearing the UFO or pressing Play aborts it (`AbortAbduction`). Everything is removed when the menu ends; the Egg and Bean cats now walk in whether or not they were met.
