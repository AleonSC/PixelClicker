## Farming pixels / Wood / Rock

**Wood is part of the Woodcutting skill now** (see the Woodcutting row; `PixelSkills.IsOptIn` is false for everything, the old opt-in switch code stays unused). **Farmer's Pack** (testing price 10 White; replaces the separate Seed and Water packs, which `MigrateOldPackData` drops; their tiers stay unlocked in old saves) rewards eight farm types (`PixelClicker.IsFarm`): **Dirt** (36, one click; old Dirt pixels near a sprout add growth speed, `dirtSeedBoost`, in `SolarSeedBoostAt`), **Seed** and **Water** (the existing pixels, no longer sold alone), **Fertilizer** (37; its click calls `Fertilize` = an instant growth jump for every sprout / Seed pet on the field), **Pollen** (38; floaty look physics; an old Pollen within `pollenReachPixels` of a popping sprout doubles its pixel's pay, `PollenNear`), **Wheat** (39; payout x (1 + `wheatPerSprout` x sprouts, max `wheatMaxSprouts`)), **Weevil** (40; while it is the pixel ON the cube, `UpdateWeevil` makes it eat one growing sprout every `weevilBiteSeconds`, `SeedSprout.EatOne`, until you click it) and **Golden Fruit** (41; a sprout grows one with `fruitSproutChance` 3% once unlocked, pays 40). The eight new types are `IsStandalone` (no potions / seeds / crafting / pets / achievements / Value upgrades); `looksVersion` 28 adds their looks; a sprout popping off gives Farming XP (`PixelSkills.GrewSprout`).

## Seed shell rework

**Seed block**: a glossy ridged seed POD (`PixelLook.blockSurface` Seed + `blob` rounded mesh, `PixelBlockTextures.SeedShell(level, max)`: ridges, dimples, a seam; cracks spread per hit with a green light leaking out; `RoundedCubeMesh` now has UVs per face; `looksVersion` 41, which also redoes Wood = rough bark on a rounded block and Stone = natural fractured rock with lichen). A cracked shell plants **1-5 seedlings** (`seedlingsMin/Max`, 0 = 1 / 5; replaces `seedsPerShell`) that grow only **plant pixels** (`PixelClicker.IsPlant` = Wheat, Pollen, Golden Fruit, Wood; `RandomPlantPixelTier` by spawn weight, falls back to any growable type when none is unlocked; `SeedSprout.PlantsOnly()`). Bought seeds and the Seed pet are unchanged (any pixel).

Looks reuse the Wood bark texture with tints (`looksVersion` 44), new `FarmAmbient` kinds Leaves / Sap / Hornets, break bursts in `PixelFarmFx.Break`, sounds `wood_chop` + `pixel_<type>` (`PixelSynth.Wood(kind, seed)`), Pixel Guide texts, bonus-bank 'good' pixels = every wood block but Twig (`PixelClicker.IsPlant` no longer includes Wood; the Inventory groups them as 'Composite: Woodcutting'). Perks: level 2 **Wood Chipper** (implemented: `Device.shopSkillId` / `shopSkillLevel` on the chipper open it in the shop and the shop search at Woodcutting 2 even though it is `vendorOnly`; `PixelConsumables.ItemVendorOnly` checks the level, `ItemSkillGated` keeps the Tinkerer from selling it) with a tutorial popup (`Perk.noticeText`, queued once per skill / level by `PixelSkills.QueuePerkNotices`, shown by `UpdatePerkNotices` when calm and tips are on; it points at Consumables and the shop search), level 3 **Seasoned wood** (old wood pixels last `woodcuttingLifespanBonus` 0 = 50% longer: `PixelSkills.SkillLifetimeFactor` read by `PixelClicker.LifetimeFactor`), level 4 Sap collector and level 6 Heart of the forest (coming soon). Saved packs are migrated (the old 'Sharp axe' level 2 perk is replaced). **Skill tree:** the first node is `axe` ('Unlock the axe to get the job done yourself', coming soon) - the axe itself is always available for now; the first person axe will be tied to this node later. `baseXp` 60, `xpGrowth` 1.45 (quicker than the others).

## Hazard blocks (PixelType.Pest 49 = Farming, GasPocket 50 = Mining; PixelClicker.IsHazard, TriggerHazard, UpdateHazardExpiry, PixelSkills.TryGetHazard / ApplyHazardPenalty / PenaltyFactor)

**Negative blocks in the skill packs**: a tough block (Pest 6 hits, Gas Pocket 5, pays nothing) that makes up a small share of the spawns (`SkillPack.hazardBaseShare` 0 = 3% at level 1, `hazardPerLevelShare` 0.2% per level, `hazardMaxShare` 8%; weights worked out in `PixelClicker.SpecialInfo` together with the filler). A few taps are harmless; the hit that would break it **sets it off** instead of paying (`CollectInternal` -> `TriggerHazard`): the Pest kills EVERY sprout (`SeedSprout.KillAll`), the Gas Pocket explodes (`FireBurstFx` + old pixels within `gasBlastWidths` (4) old-pixel widths vanish, no payout). Left alone it leaves after `hazardSeconds` (7). **Right-click defuse:** a RIGHT click on a hazard on the cube (`PixelClicker.Update` -> `DefuseHazard`, same `minClickInterval`) counts towards clearing it safely: it needs `clicksToCollect` + the hits already put in (5 to break, 2 hits in = 7 right clicks; shown as a rising '3/7', `DefusePopup`); done = no explosion / swarm, no penalty, no payout, the cube rolls a new pixel (`hazards.defused`). The auto clicker never clicks a hazard** (`CollectInternal` returns for `automatic` unless the static `HazardAutoClear` is on - the hook for a future upgrade), so the cube waits on it until you act or it goes away (the auto clicker earns only a quarter of the skill XP, which is the trade-off). **XP penalty**: each hazard set off takes `penaltyStep` (10%) off ALL skill XP (`PixelSkills.AddXp` x `PenaltyFactor`), cumulative while one is running, the `penaltySeconds` (60) timer restarting each time (`Time.time`, so it holds while paused); an event-log line says so. The pinned skill tracker shows the running penalty as a red line under it (`trackerPenalty`), only for the skill it hit (the penalty is per skill: `PenaltyFactor(id)`, the pack in play when the hazard went off). Part of the packs' pixel lists / reward tiers (`packDataVersion` 14, `looksVersion` 42), tip `hazard`, stat `hazards.triggered`. Electrician has none yet.

## Foraging pixels / Forager's Pack

**Foraging** (`PixelSkills.ForagingId`, second base gathering skill after Woodcutting; bought as the **Forager's Pack**, 150 White, `CreateForagerPack`, `packDataVersion` 18): `PixelType.Berry` 57, `Mushroom` 58, `Herb` 59, `Truffle` 60 (rare, 3 hits, pays 30), `Weeds` 61 (filler) and `Toadstool` 62 (hazard; `IsForageBlock` = Berry..Weeds, `IsHazard` / `IsStandalone` cover the Toadstool). A Toadstool that goes off (`TriggerHazard`) wipes the old pixels within `gasBlastWidths` in a spore cloud. `looksVersion` 45; FX in `PixelFarmFx` (Hit/Break cases, sound `forage_pick`), sounds `PixelSynth.Forage(kind, seed)` (`pixel_berry`..`pixel_toadstool`); `IsGood` = forage blocks except Weeds; one shop tip `pixel_Berry`. **Mold replaced the Toadstool** in the pack (`PixelType.Mold` 75, hazard + standalone, 8 clicks, `packDataVersion` 26 adds it to saved Forager's Packs, the Skills pack lists it and `hazardType = "Mold"`; the Toadstool type, look and code stay but nothing spawns it): see `PixelMold.cs` (zone that grows per click, infects fallen pixels, shake held pixels clean, right click only stops the spreading; also the glowing Berry / Mushroom / Herb / Truffle old pixels that glow from half their lifespan). Not built yet: perks' effects, a foraging vendor / machines, the later link to Seed / Farming.

## Fishing / ore rework

**Fishing** (`PixelSkills.FishingId`, third base gathering skill; bought as the **Angler's Pack**, 150 White, `CreateAnglerPack`, `packDataVersion` 19): `PixelType.Fish` 66 (2 hits, pays 4), `Seaweed` 67, `Shell` 68, `Pearl` 69 (rare, 4 hits, pays 35), `Driftwood` 70 (filler), `Jellyfish` 71 (hazard: stings away the old pixels near the cube like the Toadstool). `IsFishBlock` = Fish..Driftwood; sounds `PixelSynth.Fishing(kind, seed)` + `fish_splash`; `looksVersion` 46; tip `pixel_Fish`. **Mining ores reworked** (same migration 19): Copper, Tin, Iron, Silver 63, Gold 64 and Bedrock 65 (a random 20-30 clicks via `randomClicks`, pays 150; `packDataVersion` 20) plus **Obsidian**, which moved INTO the Prospector's Pack (15 clicks, pays 80; the separate Obsidian Pixel pack and its table entry are gone, packs that needed it now need the Prospector's Pack, saved lists are remapped). Lead / Zinc / Nickel are retired (`IsRetiredOre`, never spawn, dropped from saved Mining packs; their enum values stay for saves). Obsidian is not `IsOre` (it keeps its own FX and is not standalone) but counts as a good Mining block and sells to the Prospector vendor; it no longer has a Toggles spawn switch.


## Termite Hive, filler family (Woodcutting)
- The Hornet Nest became the **Termite Hive** (enum `PixelType.HornetNest` kept for saves; renamed in `EnsureTier`, packDataVersion 31). Breaking it (`PixelClicker.TriggerHazard`) calls `PixelTermiteSwarm.Release`: one termite per old pixel on the floor (not held, capped by `TermiteMax`), each runs to its pixel and a billboard bar over it counts down (`TermiteEatSeconds`); a left click on the pixel saves it (stats `termites.pixels_saved`), otherwise it is eaten (`ReleaseOldPixel(rb,false)`, `termites.pixels_eaten`). Pesticide Block Hold still clears the hive in one click (`clearsHazard`). Sound `termite_chitter` (`PixelHazardSynth`).
- Woodcutting's filler is a **family**: Twig (twigs tangle texture), Pinecone (enum 92) and Moss (93) share the filler's spawn share evenly (`SkillPack.extraFillerTypes`, `PixelSkills.GetFillerFamily/IsFillerType`, `PixelClicker.SwapFiller`; extras have spawn weight 0). The Shuffle bonus treats any family member as the filler. looksVersion 61 replaced the Twig / Leaf / Hive looks (new `BlockSurface.Twigs/Pinecone/Moss/LeafBlock/Hive`, `PixelBlockTextures`).

## 2026-10-15 changes

- **Foraging** is a TRULY RANDOM rotation (`SkillPack.uniformRotation`: every block of the pack in play, filler and hazards included, equally likely; `PixelSkills.TryGetUniformPool`, used by `PixelClicker.PickSpawnTierBase` and the spawn-share readout). Two hazards (`hazardType` may list several, "Mold,Toadstool", `TryParseHazard` picks one at random): **Mold** and **Toadstool**, both ONE left click to set off and ONE right click to clear (a reaction check). Mold now molds every husk on the field (`PixelMold.wholeMap`), and its timers are longer (zone hold 30 s, kill 22 s). The Toadstool gives a 12 s dizzy status (`PixelToadstoolDaze`): the cube jerks away from the cursor after each of your clicks.
- **Firemaking** (`PixelSkills.FiremakingId`, dev-only: the Firemaker's Pack, which is the old Fire Pixel test pack, hidden from the shop): Tinder, Kindling, Firewood, Flint, Ember, Fire, Charcoal, filler Ash, hazard **Wildfire** (5 hits: the fire burns up the husks around the cube; right-click 5 times to put it out). Tag `PixelTag.Firemaking` / `PixelClicker.IsFireBlock`. Mechanics still to come. Fire lost its Toggles spawn switch (it is a skill block now).
- **Mining**: Lead, Zinc and Nickel are back (no longer tagged RetiredOre; `PixelShopDefaults.ProspectorOre`), and the filler is called **Rock** everywhere (the enum was always `Rock`).
- The **Prospector's** and **Farmer's** Packs are in the shop with real prices (2,000 White + 1,000 Gray / 1,500 White + 750 Gray). Scene data is updated by `packDataVersion` 35.
