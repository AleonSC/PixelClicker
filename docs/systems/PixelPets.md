# PixelPets

Moved from CLAUDE.md. The script header comments and `CLAUDE.md` index point here.

## PixelPets.cs

Pets (added by `PixelClicker.Awake`, singleton `PixelPets.Instance`): every click (`PixelCollected`, manual or auto at `autoClickChanceFactor`) rolls `chancePerClick` (0.0003) for a pet of that pixel type (one pet per `PixelType`; owned types don't roll). A hit freezes the game (`PixelPets.PopupOpen`, `Time.timeScale = 0`, restored on close; `PixelTimeStop` doesn't undo it) behind a modal popup (canvas 750, spinning `PixelCubeIcon`, "Awesome!"; Escape closes it via `PixelWindows` priority 150; popups queue) and the pet then spawns: a rigidbody + box collider the size of the main pixel (`PixelClicker.PixelBaseSize`) wearing `CreateDisplayPixel` of its type, bouncy `PhysicMaterial`, that every `hopInterval` seconds (only from the ground) hops with a sideways + upward impulse and a rolling torque, heads back to the cube when beyond `roamRadius` or near the screen edge, is respawned above the cube if it falls out of the world, and is hidden while a takeover minigame runs (`TakeoverActive`). **Mouse:** hovering a pet stops it (`hoverStopDamping`; `PixelPets.HoveringPet`, sets `PixelClicker.ExternalClickBlock` so the cube ignores the click and `PixelGrab` skips it), (a pet that is behind the clickable cube from the camera never counts as hovered, so the cube still gets the click: `PetUnderPointer` compares ray distances) left-click picks it up (drag on the camera-facing plane, `followSharpness`, collides like Pixel Grabbing) and letting go throws it (`throwStrength`, `maxThrowSpeed`). **The Ghost pet only hovers** (`RoamGhost`: no gravity, trigger collider, drifts between random spots `ghostHoverHeight` pixel-widths above the floor with a bob and a slow spin; a thrown ghost's speed fades into the drift). **The Glass Pet** (`MaybeBreak` / `BreakAndReform`) shatters at random while it moves (`glassBreakChance` per second at `glassBreakSpeed`, faster = likelier; not while held or standing still): the pet turns invisible but stays solid in place, `PetReformShard`s fly about for `glassHoldSeconds`, then reverse back into it over `glassReformSeconds` and it reappears (`glassCooldown`); sounds `glass_shatter` / `glass_shatter_reform`. (Glass **husks** are unchanged: they still shatter on landing and are gone.) **The Seed Pet** (`RoamSeed`) is a big sprout (`seedPetScale` 3x the main pixel, `PixelLooks.CreateSproutObject` + a tall box collider) that never hops: wherever it lands or is put down it stands up, pushes up a dirt mound, goes kinematic and **plants itself** (`PlantPet`, only after a short settle time and when `SolidBelow` finds ground other than its own collider or the clicker cube; **it sprouts up out of the floor** at a spot beside the cube (`SeedPetSpawnPoint` + `FindGround`: starts hidden below the floor, rises over `seedPetRiseSeconds` (1.6) with the dirt mound staying at floor level, `Pet.rise/groundY`, planted at once; if no floor is found below, it drops from the spot as before) and ignores the cube's colliders, so it can't land and plant on top of the cube; picking it up uproots it; picking it up uproots it, `Uproot`), and while planted it sows a seed every `seedPetInterval` (10-20) s (`SowSeed`: a random spot 1.5..`seedPetRadius` (5) main-pixel widths around it, on screen, `PixelClicker.PlantSeedSproutAt` = a `SeedSprout` planted at once that grows a random pixel, capped by `seedMaxSprouts`; hyper pets sow faster). **The Vacuum Pet** (`SuckAround`) sucks up the nearest husk within `vacuumRadius` pixel-widths every `vacuumInterval` s (`ReleaseOldPixel(body, false)`: no payout, nothing else counts; they fly in and shrink) and a small camera-facing number above it (`counterText`, `LateUpdate`) shows the total (stat counter `pet.vacuum`, `PixelStats.Total(key)`). The Stats screen has a **Pets** section (found X / total, every pixel type's pet or ???, the vacuum count). **Slower despawn:** while a pet is out its pixel type's husks age `despawnSlowFactor` (2) times slower (`PixelPets.DespawnRate`, read by `OldPixelDespawn` every 0.5 s). **Spawn bonus:** while a pet is active (owned and switched on) its pixel type's spawn weight is multiplied by `spawnWeightMultiplier` (2) in `PixelClicker.PickSpawnTier` (`SpawnWeightOf` / `PixelPets.SpawnMultiplier`; potions and dev spawn selections still override). Toggles window has a fourth **Pets** tab (tick box per owned pet = `SetOn`; the Toggles button also shows once a pet exists). Saved as `SaveData.pets` ("type:off" strings; `Export/Import`). Stat `pets.found`, Stats row "Pets found". Dev tools: "Give pet (selected pixel)" button (popup; "All pixels" gives every pet quietly) and a separate "Give all pets" button (all dev gives pass `Award(type, popup, startOff: true)`: the pets arrive switched OFF), and "Give a Dragon Cube" (the lowest-numbered one you still lack).

## Which pixels have pets
`PixelPets.CanHavePet(type)`: every pixel type except the seven Dragon Cubes and the hazards (Pest, Gas Pocket, Hornet Nest, Toadstool, Jellyfish, Mold). It used to skip all standalone pixels (Fire, the ores, the skill blocks...); they have pets now. The pet find roll (`OnCollected`), the 'Pet' achievements (`PixelAchievements.RegisterPetAchievements`, one per pet type) and the Game Database's Pet entries all use it. The Collect / Click achievements follow the same idea (`PixelAchievements.NoPixelAchievements`: only Dragon Cubes and hazards are left out).


**Right-click menu (2026-10-16, `PixelPetsMenu.cs`):** right-click a pet for a small menu at the pointer with its name and 'Pet away' (`SetOn(type, false)`, turned back on in Toggles > Pets). Clicking elsewhere / Escape closes it.
