## Main menu camera / floor / welcome-back flow

**The camera swing is smoother** (`PixelCameraIntro.Apply` turns against an 'up' that blends from world up to the scene camera's own up (`upRef`), so a TOP-DOWN scene camera no longer makes the picture flip 180 degrees at the very end of the swing: yaw / pitch / distance interpolation instead of a slerp between the two offsets, one continuous look-at blend, quintic `Smoother` ease, frame time clamped to 0.05 s). The floor-fade switches are `keepFloorOnTitle` (false = fading on) and `floorFadeSeconds` (0 = 1): a serialized bool that defaults to true / a float that defaults to 1 would read false / 0 on a scene saved before the field existed - **new serialized defaults must be 'off' / 0 = default**. **Margins slide:** `PixelHud.Update` slides the top / bottom black bars (and `PixelDockedButton.Apply` pushes every docked button outward by the same amount, `PixelHud.RevealEased`, `revealSeconds` 0 = 0.9) away while `PixelTitleScreen.Showing` or `PixelTitleScreen.Reversing` (the camera swinging back) and in as the camera moves on Play. **Props fade:** old pixels and placed devices shrink away / back with `PixelFloor.propsK` (`ApplyProps`, `PropsFadeIn()` called after a save load, `PixelClicker.CollectOldPixelTransforms`): on Quit > Main menu they go first, then the floor, then the camera; on arrival the floor comes first, then they grow back. **Blur background OFF** draws the live scene unblurred at full size (`PixelTitleScreen.UpdateSharp`, a full-resolution camera render in `strongImage`). While the textured floor is hidden / fading a flat floor-coloured copy of the floor mesh sits just under it (`PixelFloor.UpdateFlatBase`, `AverageColor`, queue 1990), so the menu background is the floor's colour and the textured floor fades in from it. **Live background ON** (`PixelTitleWanderers.LiveBackground`): the main menu and the camera intro show **only the sky - no floor**: `PixelFloor.LateUpdate` fades the floor with `FloorVisibility` (0..1 over `fadeSeconds` 1, `hideFloorOnTitle`): hidden while the title screen shows or the camera is moving (`PixelCameraIntro.Moving`), so after Play the camera swings as normal and the floor fades in once it has settled; the fade draws see-through copies of the floor's materials (`PixelClicker.BuildTransparentMaterial`, `EnsureProxies` / `DropProxies`, tiling copied by `ApplyTiling`), at 0 `Renderer.forceRenderingOff` hides it (the renderer stays enabled), and the old pixels on the floor are hidden below 0.6 (`PixelClicker.SetOldPixelsHidden`; pets / devices are not). **Quit > Main menu**: `PixelTitleScreen.BeginReturn` first calls `PixelFloor.FadeOutThen(ReverseCamera)` (the floor fades out), THEN `PixelCameraIntro.PlayReverse` zooms the camera back. **Live background OFF**: no camera intro at all (`PixelCameraIntro.LateUpdate` keeps the camera at its normal pose, `PlayReverse` returns false) - the title shows the blurred floor picture and Play just fades it into the default view; the floor is never hidden. **The 'Welcome back' offline-progress window** now waits for the title screen AND the camera intro (`PixelOfflineProgress.Update` checks `PixelCameraIntro.Moving`).
