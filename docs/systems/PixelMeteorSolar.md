## PixelMeteorSolar.cs

**Meteor and Solar rework.** **Meteor** (`PixelLook.meteorTexture` + `damageCracks` + `cometTail`; tier now `clicksToCollect` 3, pays 45, `glow` off, `packDataVersion` 12 migrates saved packs / tiers, `looksVersion` 35 replaces the saved looks): a dark cratered rock (`PixelStarTextures.Meteor(level, max)`, 64 px, lava cracks that spread / brighten per hit, dull embers at level 0), a few rising embers on the cube (`PixelCometFx.Attach`), sparks + chips on every non-final hit (`PixelCometFx.Hit`, `disableMeteorFx`) and a fiery world-space wake on the husk that streaks away (`PixelCometFx.AttachWake`, added in the `fly` branch of `SpawnFallingCopy`); it still launches in a straight tapered trail and `FlyAwayLaunched` still knocks stars down. **Solar** (`PixelLook.sunTexture` + `sunFx` + custom physics with gravity 0): churning gold with a white-hot face centre (`PixelStarTextures.Sun`), a turning corona + rays billboard behind the cube (`PixelSunFx` / `PixelSunRays`, swells with daylight; also on its husks), and the husk HOVERS (`OldPixelSolarHover`: spring to ~1.7 husk widths above the floor with a bob, drifts to a stop) and pays a trickle (`TrickleFraction` 0.08 of its value every `TrickleSeconds` 4 via `AddCurrency`, x`PayMultiplier`). **Daylight** (`PixelDaylight`, added by `PixelClicker.Awake`, not saved): every Solar click (`OnSolarClick`, in `CollectInternal`) adds `perClick` (0.1), it fades `decayPerSecond` (0.012); at level L seeds / Seed pets grow faster (`GrowthBonus` = L x `seedGrowthBonus` 1.5, added in `SolarSeedBoostAt`) and hover pay x (1 + L x `hoverPayBonus` 2); a small sun + bar at the top right (canvas 105) shows while L > 0 and Solar is unlocked (`hideBar`, `disableDaylight`). Old Solar pixels last `solarLifetimeFactor` (0 = 1.6) times as long as other husks (`LifetimeFactor`, in the two normal `AddDespawn` calls of `SpawnFallingCopy`); old Solar clusters (5 touching never despawn) show a faint yellow aura: `UpdateSolarClusters` calls `OldPixelSolarAura.Keep()` on every member of a complete group and each draws a soft camera-facing glow that fades in / out (overlapping glows read as one aura). Not built: Stardust raising daylight, Water drying faster in daylight.
