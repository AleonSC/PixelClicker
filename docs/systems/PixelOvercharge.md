## PixelOvercharge.cs / PixelElectricLinks.cs / PixelBolts.cs

**Electric pixel abilities** (both components added by `PixelClicker.Awake`). **Overcharge** (`PixelOvercharge`): a click that harvests an Electric pixel (manual, or the auto clicker's own with `triggerOnAutoClicks`) gives the auto clicker a split-second burst (`burstClicks` 8 `clicker.AutoCollect()` calls over `burstSeconds` 0.6; needs the Auto Clicker bought - toggled off still counts, `needsAutoClicker`; the burst's own clicks never start another burst) while `PixelOverchargeRing` draws jagged bolts circling the cube on tilted orbits (world space, follows the cube, fades) and a flashing, shaking, popping "OVERCHARGE!" rises above it (UI canvas 640; sound `overcharge`, a code-made `PixelSynth.Charge` sweep while its clip list is empty; stat `overcharge.bursts`). **Device links** (`PixelElectricLinks`): every scan (`linkRange` = 3 main-pixel widths, only pixels in the camera view) the Electric old pixels on the floor are linked into a network growing out from the placed devices (`PixelPlacedDevice.All`): from each device DAISY CHAINS run pixel to pixel (`Attach` / `NearestUnlinked`: every pixel links to the nearest unlinked one in reach, then that one to the next; new chains start from the device while pixels remain in reach; pixels keep their old partner via `stickiness`, `maxLinks`); Electric pixels out of reach of any device still daisy-chain to each other (a chain grows from both ends), **Battery model:** pixels linked to a timed device POWER it (`PixelPlacedDevice.HoldPower` / `Powered`: its countdown pauses, the timer text shows POWERED; uses-mode devices just get arcs). **Every pixel that arcs to another (to a device or only to each other, `Register`) gets `pixelCharge` (100) charge and never ages while linked** (a free chain's first pixel is tracked in `chainSeeds`); only device groups drain. The group of a device is the pool and `DrainAndHold` drains `drainPerSecond` (25) from ONE pixel at a time, the one FURTHEST along its daisy chain from the device (`ChainDepth`, ties -> older), so chains burn from the far end and the pixel touching the machine goes last; it flickers / sparks below `fizzleFraction` and `Dissipate`s (`OldPixelDespawn.Begin`) at 0; new pixels add to the pool without interrupting; linked pixels never age (`KeepAlive`) and stay when the device ends. **At most `maxLinkedTotal` (30) pixels are linked in TOTAL across all groups** (3 + 10 = 13 used): `EvictOldest` despawns the oldest beyond that (out with the old, new ones are safe until they are the oldest). The old +1 s per pixel / +10 s chain life / vanish-with-device rules are gone. **the Electric pet is a rechargeable battery bank** (`PixelPets.TryGetActivePetBody`: owned, switched on, shown, not shattered; its Rigidbody joins the daisy-chain network as one more node, never despawned, not counted in the 30-cap, never eaten by a Charge Booster): while it is part of a device's group the group drains the pet's own charge (`petCapacity` 600, `petCharge`, same `drainPerSecond`) BEFORE any pixel, and once it is empty the pixels drain as usual; it recharges `petRechargePerSecond` (2 = about 5 minutes) only while it powers nothing, and a tiny camera-facing meter floats over it (`showPetMeter`, `DrawPetMeter`; the charge is not saved, it starts full); the pet stands still while it is linked (`PixelElectricLinks.PetConnected`); The arcs are one dynamic mesh redrawn every `redrawInterval`. `PixelBolts` = shared mesh builder for jagged crossed-ribbon bolts (`Jagged`, `Strokes`; glow + core passes; vertex colours for `PixelLooks.OverlayMaterial`).
