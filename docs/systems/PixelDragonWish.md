## PixelDragonWish.cs

**Dragon Cubes + the wish.** Seven rare-drop tiers (`PixelType.DragonCube1..7`, `PixelTier.rareDrop`, helpers `PixelClicker.IsDragonCube/DragonCubeStars`; created by `PixelClicker.EnsureDragonCubes`, switch `disableDragonCubes`, weight `dragonCubeSpawnWeight` (0 = default 0.0001 each, ~1 in 70,000 blocks each)) spawn with no unlock - since 2026-10-16 every rare drop rolls ON ITS OWN before the normal pick at a FIXED chance per block = weight / `rareReferenceWeight` (7), so the odds no longer depend on how many pixel types are unlocked (`PixelClicker.TryPickRare`; before, with only White unlocked, a Dragon Cube was ~1 in 1,400) (`CanSpawn` / `GetClickTierIndex` honour `rareDrop`), pay 1 of their own currency, show in the Inventory / currency panel only once held, and are kept out of the unlock chain, Value upgrades, potions, crafting (recipes, crafting grid, combos), pets and achievements (`IsDragonCube` / `rareDrop` filters). Look (`PixelLook.starCubes` 1-7 + `starColor`, `PixelLooks.StarCubesMesh`): glassy orange translucent cube with a thin outline and 1-7 dark orange small cubes inside laid out like dragon-ball stars (unlit vertex-coloured mesh via `OverlayMaterial`, so they show through the body; looks added by `looksVersion` 10). First find of each: event-log line + tip `dragon_first`; holding all seven (`PixelClicker.HasAllDragonCubes`) shows a pulsing "Summon the Dragon" button under the top bar. **Summon** (`SummonRoutine`, all unscaled time): uses up the seven cubes, `Time.timeScale` 0, sky dims, seven block-drawn cubes rise into a ring, orbit faster with lightning flickers, spiral together in a white flash, then a follow-the-leader cube dragon (`BuildDragon` / `AnimateDragon`: body segments sit where the head was `lag` ago; head with mane, horns, eye, whiskers) bursts out; it offers three random **wishes** (`PickWishes`): **Fortune** (`fortuneSeconds` of current income at once), **Golden Hour** (`PixelClicker.WishMultiplier` x3 payouts for 10 min), **Ultra Blessing** (5 Ultra on random unlocked types), **Potion Stock** (3 random potions), **A New Friend** (an unowned pet via `PixelPets.Award(type, false)`); **the first of the three wishes is always 'Dragon Friend'** (`GiveDragonPet`: a Dragon Cube pet - `DragonCube1..7` are valid pet types - you don't own yet, offered until all seven are owned) and the other two are random. Escape does nothing meanwhile (registered window). Sound `dragon_summon` (code-synthesized `PixelSynth.Summon` while its clip list is empty); stats `dragon.summons/wishes`. Added by `PixelClicker.Awake`.
