# PixelVisitorMinigame

Moved from CLAUDE.md. The script header comments and `CLAUDE.md` index point here.

## PixelVisitorMinigame.cs / PixelTinkererMinigame.cs / PixelWizardMinigame.cs / PixelEntrepreneurMinigame.cs / PixelFarmerMinigame.cs

**Visitor minigames** (no shop packs any more - started by `PixelGuideVendor`; every `minInterval..maxInterval` s after `firstVisitDelay` (+ `StartDelayOffset`), `AllowAnother`; dev Spawn/Despawn work). `PixelVisitorMinigame` (abstract) holds everything: a code-drawn block-built visitor (`DrawVisitor(art)` in a 300x400 space via `Block`) waits for a **calm moment** (`CalmForVisit`: no Got it box showing or queued (`PixelHints.TipsPending`), no dragon show / wish / pet popup, not paused / Time Stop; the check runs every frame once the timer is up) and for the **shared cooldown** (`sharedCooldownSeconds` 180 after ANY vendor leaves, static `nextAnyVisitTime` / `anyVisiting`), visits every `visitMinSeconds..visitMaxSeconds` (480-900 s; renamed fields so old serialized 300-600 are dropped), then **darkens the screen (`visitDarkness`) and stops time (`Freeze`, `Time.timeScale` 0, restored by `Unfreeze`; left alone if already stopped) from the moment they appear**, slides in from the right edge (`slideSeconds`) and a speech box greets the player with an offer at `discountFraction` (0.8) off (`Wares` = items where `IsWare(item)`, `PriceOf` = `ItemCosts` x (1 - discount), min 1 per line, paid like the shop incl. minigame currencies). **Accept** opens the wares window (name, discounted cost coloured by affordability, owned / capacity, Buy x1, Full at the hold limit; `RefreshWares`), **Reject** or Escape (`PixelWindows` priority 150) sends them away; time resumes as they slide out. Texts are Inspector fields (empty = the visitor's `Default*`). **Every vendor is a CUBE character** now (Cubie's shape): `CubeBody(a, face, top, side, shoe)` draws the legs / shoes / arms / shaded cube and `CubeEyes` the eyes, and each vendor adds their trade on top - Wizard (purple, pointy star hat, long beard, glowing staff), Tinkerer (copper, goggles, cap, gear backpack, wrench), Farmer (leaf green, straw hat, sideburns, wheat stalk, shotgun, seed sack), Entrepreneur (navy suit cube, tie, briefcase, coin cube), the Prospector (orange, hard hat, pickaxe), and the cats Egg (orange cube cat holding the egg) and Bean (grey tabby cube cat with a sun hat and bean pouch). A new visitor = a subclass overriding `Id`, `IsWare`, `DrawVisitor`, `DefaultName/Greeting/WaresTitle` + a "Minigames" line in `PixelSystems.All` (no shop pack: `PixelGuideVendor.StartVendors` starts it). **Tinkerer** sells consumable devices, `devicesOffered` (3) random ones per visit with `stockMin..stockMax` (1-3) each; **Wizard** sells potions (every potion whose pixel is unlocked or owned; combo potions are craft-only) but only `potionsOffered` (3) random ones per visit with `stockMin..stockMax` (1-3) bottles each (base hooks `OfferedWares` / `StockMin` / `StockMax`, per-visit `stock`, 'Sold out' button, 'Stock n' text); **Farmer** (`PixelFarmerMinigame`, id `farmer`, block-drawn: straw hat, overalls, pitchfork, seed sack) sells SEEDS (`IsWare` = seed items of unlocked pixel types) `seedsOffered` (5) random ones per visit; stock by rarity via the `StockFor(item)` hook (spawn weight >= 1: `commonStock` 10-30, 0.3-1: `uncommonStock` 4-10, rarer: `rareStock` 1-3); with `dragonSeedChance` (5%) per visit he also brings a **Dragon Seed** (`ModifyWares` hook, listed first, stock 1, only while the player lacks a Dragon Cube; `Highlighted(item)` hook -> `PixelPulseGlow`: the row pulses gold with a glowing outline and yellow name): a Seed device with `seedType` DragonCube1 as marker (`PixelConsumables.IsDragonSeed`, typed costs 500K White + 250K Black, hold 3, not in the shop's Seeds card); planting it picks `PixelClicker.PickMissingDragonCube()` (a Dragon Cube the player doesn't hold; refuses when all seven are held) and the sprout's pop-off gives that cube straight away (`AddCurrency`, first-find announcement included; the flying pixel is for show, amount 0). He is drawn with black hair, wispy facial hair, a shotgun held upright at his side and a wheat stalk in his mouth; **when he brings a Dragon Seed a second speech box (`dragonAsideText`, base `AsideText` / `asidePanel`, 'Next' button) shows after you accept**, and the Dev Tools tick box 'Farmer always has dragon seeds' (`PixelFarmerMinigame.DevAlwaysDragonSeeds`, pref `Dev.FarmerSeeds`) forces the seed on every visit; **Pixel Entrepreneur** (`PixelEntrepreneurMinigame`, id `entrepreneur`) overrides `RollTrades()` instead of `IsWare`: `offerCount` (3) pixel-for-pixel deals, each asks `minPayFraction..maxPayFraction` of a held pixel type (>= `minHeld`) and gives another unlocked type at a rarity-based rate (`UnitValue` = 1 / (spawnWeight x amountPerClick / clicksToCollect)) times a random luck `minLuck..maxLuck`; the base builds a trade row (`Trade`, `DoTrade`, button 'Trade' then 'Sold') when `RollTrades` returns a list. Counters `<id>.bought`, `shop.items`; sounds `<id>_spawn` (+ automatic `<id>_click`).

## Peek (2026-10-15)

Every visitor (all PixelVisitorMinigame subclasses: Tinkerer, Wizard, Entrepreneur, Farmer, Lumberjack, Prospector, Egg, Bean; Cubie is separate and keeps his full visits) now PEEKS first (`usePeek`, on): they slide ~40% in from the right edge, leaning in, with a small speech bubble (`peekText`, '{0}' = name) and an X, while the game keeps running (no dim, no freeze, not registered with PixelWindows yet). Clicking the visitor or the bubble starts the normal full visit from there (screen darkens, time stops, greeting, wares). The X, or `peekSeconds` (20) without an answer (time in a menu doesn't count, PixelCalm.InMenu), sends them away: an event-log line (`missedLog`) and they come back after `missedReturnFactor` (0.5) of the normal wait (the shared cooldown is shortened the same way). The click catcher (a clear Image + Button on the visitor) is removed as soon as the peek ends.

**2026-10-16:** the Farmer only visits while Farming is the skill in training (`CanVisit`, like the Lumberjack with Woodcutting). Vendors can show an orange '!' with a tooltip on their Toggles row (`PixelMinigame.ToggleNote`: the Lumberjack and the Farmer say when they visit). The two rare cats (Egg, Bean) can't be switched off (`PixelMinigame.CanSwitchOff` false: no Toggles row; a saved 'off' is switched back on).
**Shared training rule (2026-10-16 cleanup):** a vendor that only comes while one skill trains overrides `VisitSkillId` (Lumberjack = Woodcutting, Farmer = Farming, Prospector = Mining); the base `CanVisit` checks `PixelSkills.IsTraining(id)` and the base `ToggleNote` writes '<name> only visits while you are training <skill>.' for its Toggles row.

## Jabs at Cubie (2026-10-23, Cubie's bill)
Once Cubie's shop is open (visit 2 done), a visit's greeting ends with a jab at Cubie `cubieJabChance` (35%) of the time, in small italics
(`GreetingWithJab`). The built-in jabs are ONE table in `PixelVisitorMinigame.CubieJabs(id)` (Lumberjack, Prospector, Wizard, Tinkerer,
Entrepreneur, Farmer: two each); a visitor's own `cubieJabs` Inspector list wins. The vendors resent his monopoly, so the player ends up on their side.

**Vendors buying goods - one shared rule (2026-10-24):** every vendor that buys goods for pure currency prices them with `PixelMarket` (Quote / RecordSale / TrendText): the clicks the goods took x what a click on pure blocks earns x the route rate (raw skill currency 1.2x; products 2x and orders 3x when they exist), less as that vendor's market for the good fills up (1 / (1 + clicks-worth sold / 900), floor 25%, half recovers every 10 real minutes). The Lumberjack's Sell tab shows 'Market N% (recovering)' beside the amount; the Prospector's offer rows show it on their second line (base hooks `OnTradeDone`, `TradeNote`). The old per-vendor copies of the rarity formula (sell rate 0.8, the Lumberjack's bulk discount) are gone; the Entrepreneur's pixel-for-pixel deals use `PixelMarket.RarityValue`. Foraging, Fishing and Farming have no buying vendor yet.
