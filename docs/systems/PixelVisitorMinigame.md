## PixelVisitorMinigame.cs / PixelTinkererMinigame.cs / PixelWizardMinigame.cs / PixelEntrepreneurMinigame.cs / PixelFarmerMinigame.cs

**Visitor minigames** (no shop packs any more - started by `PixelGuideVendor`; every `minInterval..maxInterval` s after `firstVisitDelay` (+ `StartDelayOffset`), `AllowAnother`; dev Spawn/Despawn work). `PixelVisitorMinigame` (abstract) holds everything: a code-drawn block-built visitor (`DrawVisitor(art)` in a 300x400 space via `Block`) waits for a **calm moment** (`CalmForVisit`: no Got it box showing or queued (`PixelHints.TipsPending`), no dragon show / wish / pet popup, not paused / Time Stop; the check runs every frame once the timer is up) and for the **shared cooldown** (`sharedCooldownSeconds` 180 after ANY vendor leaves, static `nextAnyVisitTime` / `anyVisiting`), visits every `visitMinSeconds..visitMaxSeconds` (480-900 s; renamed fields so old serialized 300-600 are dropped), then **darkens the screen (`visitDarkness`) and stops time (`Freeze`, `Time.timeScale` 0, restored by `Unfreeze`; left alone if already stopped) from the moment they appear**, slides in from the right edge (`slideSeconds`) and a speech box greets the player with an offer at `discountFraction` (0.8) off (`Wares` = items where `IsWare(item)`, `PriceOf` = `ItemCosts` x (1 - discount), min 1 per line, paid like the shop incl. minigame currencies). **Accept** opens the wares window (name, discounted cost coloured by affordability, owned / capacity, Buy x1, Full at the hold limit; `RefreshWares`), **Reject** or Escape (`PixelWindows` priority 150) sends them away; time resumes as they slide out. Texts are Inspector fields (empty = the visitor's `Default*`). **Every vendor is a CUBE character** now (Cubie's shape): `CubeBody(a, face, top, side, shoe)` draws the legs / shoes / arms / shaded cube and `CubeEyes` the eyes, and each vendor adds their trade on top - Wizard (purple, pointy star hat, long beard, glowing staff), Tinkerer (copper, goggles, cap, gear backpack, wrench), Farmer (leaf green, straw hat, sideburns, wheat stalk, shotgun, seed sack), Entrepreneur (navy suit cube, tie, briefcase, coin cube), the Prospector (orange, hard hat, pickaxe), and the cats Egg (orange cube cat holding the egg) and Bean (grey tabby cube cat with a sun hat and bean pouch). A new visitor = a subclass overriding `Id`, `IsWare`, `DrawVisitor`, `DefaultName/Greeting/WaresTitle` + a "Minigames" line in `PixelSystems.All` (no shop pack: `PixelGuideVendor.StartVendors` starts it). **Tinkerer** sells consumable devices, `devicesOffered` (3) random ones per visit with `stockMin..stockMax` (1-3) each; **Wizard** sells potions (every potion whose pixel is unlocked or owned; combo potions are craft-only) but only `potionsOffered` (3) random ones per visit with `stockMin..stockMax` (1-3) bottles each (base hooks `OfferedWares` / `StockMin` / `StockMax`, per-visit `stock`, 'Sold out' button, 'Stock n' text); **Farmer** (`PixelFarmerMinigame`, id `farmer`, block-drawn: straw hat, overalls, pitchfork, seed sack) sells SEEDS (`IsWare` = seed items of unlocked pixel types) `seedsOffered` (5) random ones per visit; stock by rarity via the `StockFor(item)` hook (spawn weight >= 1: `commonStock` 10-30, 0.3-1: `uncommonStock` 4-10, rarer: `rareStock` 1-3); with `dragonSeedChance` (5%) per visit he also brings a **Dragon Seed** (`ModifyWares` hook, listed first, stock 1, only while the player lacks a Dragon Cube; `Highlighted(item)` hook -> `PixelPulseGlow`: the row pulses gold with a glowing outline and yellow name): a Seed device with `seedType` DragonCube1 as marker (`PixelConsumables.IsDragonSeed`, typed costs 500K White + 250K Black, hold 3, not in the shop's Seeds card); planting it picks `PixelClicker.PickMissingDragonCube()` (a Dragon Cube the player doesn't hold; refuses when all seven are held) and the sprout's pop-off gives that cube straight away (`AddCurrency`, first-find announcement included; the flying pixel is for show, amount 0). He is drawn with black hair, wispy facial hair, a shotgun held upright at his side and a wheat stalk in his mouth; **when he brings a Dragon Seed a second speech box (`dragonAsideText`, base `AsideText` / `asidePanel`, 'Next' button) shows after you accept**, and the Dev Tools tick box 'Farmer always has dragon seeds' (`PixelFarmerMinigame.DevAlwaysDragonSeeds`, pref `Dev.FarmerSeeds`) forces the seed on every visit; **Pixel Entrepreneur** (`PixelEntrepreneurMinigame`, id `entrepreneur`) overrides `RollTrades()` instead of `IsWare`: `offerCount` (3) pixel-for-pixel deals, each asks `minPayFraction..maxPayFraction` of a held pixel type (>= `minHeld`) and gives another unlocked type at a rarity-based rate (`PixelMarket.RarityValue`, the shared value ladder) times a random luck `minLuck..maxLuck`; the base builds a trade row (`Trade`, `DoTrade`, button 'Trade' then 'Sold') when `RollTrades` returns a list. Counters `<id>.bought`, `shop.items`; sounds `<id>_spawn` (+ automatic `<id>_click`).

## Peek (2026-10-15)

Every visitor (all PixelVisitorMinigame subclasses: Tinkerer, Wizard, Entrepreneur, Farmer, Lumberjack, Prospector, Egg, Bean; Cubie is separate and keeps his full visits) now PEEKS first (`usePeek`, on): they slide ~40% in from the right edge, leaning in, with a small speech bubble (`peekText`, '{0}' = name) and an X, while the game keeps running (no dim, no freeze, not registered with PixelWindows yet). Clicking the visitor or the bubble starts the normal full visit from there (screen darkens, time stops, greeting, wares). The X, or `peekSeconds` (20) without an answer (time in a menu doesn't count, PixelCalm.InMenu), sends them away: an event-log line (`missedLog`) and they come back after `missedReturnFactor` (0.5) of the normal wait (the shared cooldown is shortened the same way). The click catcher (a clear Image + Button on the visitor) is removed as soon as the peek ends.

**2026-10-16:** the Farmer only visits while Farming is the skill in training (`CanVisit`, like the Lumberjack with Woodcutting). Vendors can show an orange '!' with a tooltip on their Toggles row (`PixelMinigame.ToggleNote`: the Lumberjack and the Farmer say when they visit). The two rare cats (Egg, Bean) can't be switched off (`PixelMinigame.CanSwitchOff` false: no Toggles row; a saved 'off' is switched back on).
**Shared training rule (2026-10-16 cleanup):** a vendor that only comes while one skill trains overrides `VisitSkillId` (Lumberjack = Woodcutting, Farmer = Farming, Prospector = Mining); the base `CanVisit` checks `PixelSkills.IsTraining(id)` and the base `ToggleNote` writes '<name> only visits while you are training <skill>.' for its Toggles row.

## Jabs at Cubie (2026-10-23, Cubie's bill)
Once Cubie's shop is open (visit 2 done), a visit's greeting ends with a jab at Cubie `cubieJabChance` (35%) of the time, in small italics
(`GreetingWithJab`). The built-in jabs are ONE table in `PixelVisitorMinigame.CubieJabs(id)` (Lumberjack, Prospector, Wizard, Tinkerer,
Entrepreneur, Farmer: two each); a visitor's own `cubieJabs` Inspector list wins. The vendors resent his monopoly, so the player ends up on their side.

**Vendors buying goods - one shared rule (2026-10-24):** every vendor that buys goods for pure currency prices them with `PixelMarket` (Quote / RecordSale / TrendText): the clicks the goods took x what a click on pure blocks earns x the route rate (raw skill currency 1.2x; products 2x and orders 3x when they exist), less as that vendor's market for the good fills up (1 / (1 + clicks-worth sold / 900), floor 25%, half recovers every 10 real minutes). The Lumberjack's Sell tab shows 'Market N% (recovering)' beside the amount; the Prospector's offer rows show it on their second line (base hooks `OnTradeDone`, `TradeNote`). The old per-vendor copies of the rarity formula (sell rate 0.8, the Lumberjack's bulk discount) are gone; the Entrepreneur's pixel-for-pixel deals use `PixelMarket.RarityValue`.

**Goods buyers and calling (2026-10-24, PixelVisitorMinigameGoods.cs):** a vendor with `BuysGoods` buys one skill's goods (`GoodsSkillId`: that skill pack's types that pay something) through `RollGoodsTrades` (its own `GoodsOffers` Inspector block: offers, share of your stock, luck, min held), priced by `PixelMarket`; rows show the market trend, accepting records the sale. Buyers: the Prospector (Mining), the Forager 'Hazel' (Foraging), the Angler 'Old Salt' (Fishing) - each visits only while its skill is trained - and the Farmer's new Sell tab (Farming; his Buy tab keeps the seeds); the Lumberjack keeps his own Sell panel on the same market. **Calling:** every vendor who buys from you (`BuysFromPlayer`) has a 'Call' button on its Toggles row (Vendors tab): he comes at the next calm moment whatever skill is training, skipping the gap after another vendor. One call per `PixelGuideVendor.vendorCallCooldownMinutes` (30) of PLAY time, shared by all vendors (stat `vendor.callReady`, so it is saved); the button shows the minutes left, then 'Coming' until he arrives. The random visits carry on as before.

**Specialty goods, the shared Sell view and the Wizard's trades (2026-10-24):** every vendor who buys from you (`BuysFromPlayer`: a skill goods buyer, or a vendor with specialty goods) has the SAME Sell view (PixelVisitorMinigameSell.cs, was Big Tim's panel): pick the good, the amount (slider + 10 / 25 / 50% / All) and the pure colour; the button shows the live price ('Sell for 1.2K Red') and the line above it his market trend. A vendor with no wares (Prospector, Forager, Angler) opens straight on it. Goods = his skill's currencies (raw rate 1.2x) + his SPECIALTY goods from `PixelMarket`'s one table (vendor, minigame material, rate, effort clicks per unit): Wizard - Ectoplasm (ghost, 2x, 6 clicks), Magic Wood (eviltree, 2x, 5), Stardust (2x, 20); Prospector - Meteor Chunks (meteor, 2x, 1.5); Tinkerer - Bomb Parts (bomb, 2x, 10); Big Tim - Magic Wood (1x, 5). Materials are read / spent only through `PixelMaterials.CostHeld / CostSpend` (a minigame id now means its MATERIAL: `PixelMinigame.MaterialAmount / TrySpendMaterial`; the Ghost spends Ectoplasm, not ghosts caught). Price = rate x PurePerClick x effort clicks x saturation (averaged over the amount) x his mood / roll (`moodRange`, `offerRollRange`, base Inspector) x `OfferBonus` (Big Tim: Woodcutting level). The Wizard's TRADES (`PixelWizardMinigame.trades`, Inspector): 20 Ectoplasm -> 1 Ghost Bait, 5 Stardust -> 1 Ultra pixel - shown as 'Trade 20 Ectoplasm for Ghost Bait' buttons beside the sell price, greyed while you can't hold more. Spending meteor chunks / stardust / Magic Wood also lowers their minigame's goal counter (they are the same number), so selling competes with saving for the packs those goals open.

Guard rail (rough, default minigame timings, only one minigame busy at a time plus breathers): selling everything a minigame drops pays about +8-14% of an hour's clicking income per minigame (ghost ~26 rounds/h x 2.5 Ectoplasm, Evil Tree ~11 trees x 9 wood, meteor ~21 x ~20 chunks, bomb ~14 x 3 parts), and the time spent playing those rounds is clicking not done, so the NET gain is roughly +4-6% each; saturation (900 clicks-worth halves a vendor's price) caps a big dump. **Vendor rapport (2026-10-24, PixelRapport.cs + PixelVisitorMinigameRapport.cs):** every vendor has a rapport meter, five steps Stranger (0) -> Customer (10) -> Regular (35) -> Friend (80) -> Partner (150 points), saved in `GameState.rapport`. ONE step table (`PixelRapport.steps`, Inspector): each step gives + sale price (0 / 3 / 6 / 10 / 15%, in `PixelMarket.Quote`), faster market recovery (1 / 1.15 / 1.3 / 1.6 / 2x, PixelMarket's half-life divided), more visits (wait x 1 / 0.95 / 0.9 / 0.8 / 0.7), bigger / rarer stock (extra kinds offered 0 / 0 / 1 / 1 / 2, extra of each 0 / 0 / 0 / 1 / 2, rare chance x 1 / 1 / 1.25 / 1.5 / 2 - the Farmer's Dragon Seed). Points: opening a visit 2; dealing - selling 1 per 150 clicks-worth (specialty goods x2), buying a ware 1, a trade 2 - at most 8 a visit; GIFTS from the Sell view's Gift button (a gift = 120 clicks' worth of the picked good, at least 1): a loved good 5, any other 1, only 3 gifts a visit count. A loved good he doesn't buy is listed as 'gift only' (heart in the list). Per-vendor DATA (`PixelRapport.vendors`): loves (Big Tim: Heartwood, Magic Wood; Wizard: Ectoplasm, Stardust; Tinkerer: Bomb Parts, Charcoal; Prospector: Gold, Meteor Chunks; Forager: Truffle; Angler: Pearl; Farmer: Golden Fruit; the Entrepreneur and the cats have no Sell view, so no gifts - they earn from visits and trades) and a greeting line per step (at Partner they let slip something about Cubie). No decay: after 60 play minutes without dealing his points trickle down 4 an hour, never below the step reached. Shown: a small HEART BADGE in the header of his greeting box and his wares / Sell window (UI map N21.4; the title is just his name; the heart takes his step's colour) - hovering or clicking it shows the RAPPORT CARD under the header (his step in its colour '♥ Regular' and '42 / 80 to Friend', a bar of four rounded segments, one per step after Stranger, each filling in its step's colour (`PixelRapport` Step.color), and the step names under them), his step line under the greeting, and his Game Database Character entry ('Rapport: Regular (42 / 80 to Friend)', 'Loves (as a gift): ...'). A step up is announced in the event log. Not built yet: the unique unlockable per vendor (proposed in Todo.txt).

**Relics - collectibles you work for (2026-10-24, PixelRelics.cs, the Relics family in PixelRareSets):** the unique unlockable per vendor is a RELIC, a collectible like the Dragon Cubes (a pixel tier, one of each, its own Collection Log section 'Relics' and gallery row) that never drops: the vendor hands it over when your rapport reaches its step (`PixelRapport` Vendor `reward` / `rewardStep`; `GrantReward` runs on the step up and at each visit's start). The Database Character entry says which relic a vendor gives and at which step. The other vendors' relics are proposals in Todo.txt.
