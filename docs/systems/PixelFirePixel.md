## PixelFirePixel.cs / PixelProspector.cs

**Fire pixel + Prospector's Pack** (`PixelType.Fire` 25, ores `Copper..Nickel` 26-31; `PixelClicker.IsOre` / **`IsStandalone`** = no integration: these types get no potions, seeds, crafting recipes / combos, pets, achievements, Value upgrades, Entrepreneur trades or guide gifts - the filters are at each of those sites; they still spawn, pay, show in the Inventory / Log / currency panel). **Fire** (testing pixel, pack 'Fire Pixel': 5 White, no requirements; look = glowing orange + `PixelLook.flames` (`PixelFlames.Attach`: a stream of flame squares rising off the cube) + `burn`; tier glow): its click calls `FireBurstFx.Play` (a horizontal ring of flame squares flying outwards from the cube, sparks, an expanding `PixelFxRing` shock ring and a light flash, sound `fire_burst`); its old pixel gets `OldPixelBurn` (falls normally while it slowly shrinks away over `fireBurnSeconds` (0 = 6) shedding embers and crackling (`fire_crackle`), doesn't age, then is released with no payout); `disableFireFx`. **Prospector's Pack** (10 White, no requirements): six ore tiers, tough (`clicksToCollect` 2-5) with `PixelLook.oreTexture` (`PixelLooks.OreTexture`: 64 px pixel-art grey rock with veins / flecks of `oreColor` metal, dark cracks spreading per hit via `damageCracks`); every non-final hit shows `OreFx.Hit` (rock chips + metal flecks, sound `ore_chip`), the break `OreFx.Break` (rocks, nuggets, dust; the ore's own `pixel_<ore>` click sound is a synthesized break); **rich vein**: a fresh ore is a rich vein with `richVeinChance` (0 = 8%) - it glitters (`OreFx.Glitter`) and pays `richVeinMultiplier` (0 = 5) x with a gold burst, shock ring and `ore_gleam` (first one per session is logged); ore old pixels clink when they land (`pixel_bounce_<ore>`); `disableProspectorFx`. **Prospector mode** (chosen as the Mining skill in the Skills window, no longer a Toggles row: `PixelSkills.PackSetting` == Mining): **the ores are NOT in the random spawns unless the mode is on** (`PixelProspector.LeftOut(type, prospecting)` in `PickSpawnTier`: mode off = ores never spawn; mode on = White, Gray, Black, Red, Green, Blue, Glass and Luminescent (`IsBase`) are left out and the ores spawn instead), `PixelClicker.RefreshSpawnTier()` re-rolls at once. All sounds are code-synthesized while their clip list is empty (`PixelAudio.SynthFor`: `PixelSynth.FireBurst/FireCrackle/OreChip/OreBreak/OreGleam/OreClink`). Shared particle helper `PixelFx.Make(parent, name, Spec)` (little squares, Sprites/Default).

**PixelFx burst pool (2026-10-19).** One-shot hit / break effects (Fire burst, ore chips / break, farm / wood / fish / forage, electric, Obsidian, treasure chest, meteor hits) use `PixelFx.Rent(centre, seconds)` + `burst.Add(name, spec)` instead of a new GameObject + ParticleSystems per hit; a runner (`PixelFxPoolRunner`) returns them after 'seconds' (at most 32 spare kept). `Configure` fully resets a reused system (incl. shape scale, one burst without allocating); `FadeGradient` results and size curves are cached (assign them, never change their keys). The click pop in `PixelBasicFx` uses the same pool (its own cap: 6 pops at once).
