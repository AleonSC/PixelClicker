# PixelTitleScreen

Moved from CLAUDE.md. The script header comments and `CLAUDE.md` index point here.

## PixelTitleScreen.cs

Start screen (added by `PixelClicker.Awake`): freezes the game (`Time.timeScale = 0`, `PixelTitleScreen.Showing`; auto clicker and the intro wait) behind a **live** blurred picture of the scene (each `LateUpdate` the main camera is rendered into a small RT and blitted down a chain: `liveDivisor`, `lightBlurDivisor`, `strongBlurDivisor`, no post-processing; the cube keeps spinning because `PixelClicker.AnimDelta` is unscaled while the title shows) with a runtime-drawn pixel-art **"PIXEL CLICKER" logo** (5x7 bitmap font, bevelled cells + shadow, `logoColors`, bobs), a black bar across the middle with a Play button and, in ONE row under it (together as wide as Play), three icon buttons drawn in code by `PixelMenuIcons` (32 px pixel art): **Load** = a floppy disk, **Settings** = a cog, **Quit** = an open door (red); `MakeIconButton`, hovering one shows its name in the small tip (`hoveredText`). Load / Settings call `PixelPauseMenu.OpenLoadFromTitle/OpenSettingsFromTitle` (the pause menu opens its view with `openedFromTitle`; Back / Escape return to the title; the title canvas drops to sorting 400 and hides its `Foreground` while the pause menu is open; the pause menu doesn't touch `timeScale` while the title shows). Play: buttons shrink + logo fades, bar thins while the strong then light blur fade out, then after `startDelay` time resumes. The component now stays after Play (canvas hidden, textures released): `PixelTitleScreen.ReturnToMenu()` (pause menu Quit > Main menu) closes open windows, runs `PixelCameraIntro.PlayReverse`, then `Open(true)` fades the title back in by running the Play transition (`ApplyTransition(t)`) backwards. Escape does nothing while it shows (`PixelWindows` entry). It shows again after Restart (scene reload = fresh launch); `showTitleScreen` turns it off. The hint system's startup quiet period uses `Time.timeSinceLevelLoad` (frozen while the title shows), and the intro tips start `introDelay` seconds after Play. Sound `game_start`. **Bar glow** (shared helper `PixelBarGlow.cs`; the HUD's top / bottom bars get the same glow on their inner edges, fields in `PixelHud`; player switch Settings > 'Neon bar glow' = `PixelHud.BarGlow`, PlayerPrefs `PixelClicker.Setting.BarGlow`, synced per save, covers the title bar too) (`barGlow`, `glowSize/InnerSize/Strength/ColorSpeed/EdgeOffset/Saturation`: `BuildBarGlow` / `UpdateBarGlow`, soft gradient strips outside + inside both bar edges and a bright LED line, hue cycles slowly on unscaled time, fades as the bar thins). Small floor (right of Play) and sky (left of Play) pickers: **drop-downs** (a `PixelUIKit.CreateDropdown` listing every look, small caption above, fade in while the mouse is near or the list is open; `showLookPickers`, `lookBoxHeight`, `lookDropdownWidth`, ...; refreshed each frame when the look changes). Settings > Floor style and Sky each have a pop-out button (`PixelPauseMenu.OpenLookPopout`).

**Background crossfade:** `UpdateBackgroundFade` (called first in `UpdateBlur`) notices when 'Live background' or 'Blur background' is switched, keeps the picture that was showing as a snapshot (`fadeTexture`, shown by `fadeImage` above the blur layers) and fades it away over about 1.1 s, so the floor / sky comes in gradually instead of swapping at once. The in-game floor itself already fades with `PixelFloor.FloorVisibility`. The blur slider toggle ignores the click that comes when you let go of the slider (`Toggle.sliderUsed` + a pointer-over-slider check).
