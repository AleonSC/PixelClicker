## PixelPadMinigame.cs

**Ultra Holes** (the reworked Ultra Pad; id `pad`, pack `unlocksMinigame = "pad"`, Minigames tab, needs the RGB pack; NOT a takeover minigame): every `minInterval..maxInterval` s (and `AllowAnother`) FOUR round holes open in the floor, one near each corner of the screen (`CornerSpot`: the viewport corner `cornerInset` inside the black bars, nudged inwards until the whole hole is on screen and `AreaFree`; at least two are needed; one `ClaimArea` per hole via `claimOwners`), each a dark rim + black void + deeper black disc (`BuildHole`, radius `holeRadiusPixels` 1.3 main-pixel widths). They stay open `roundSeconds` (45); a timer line shows at the top of the screen (`hudFormat`). **One hole is the correct one**, marked by a spinning `CreateDisplayPixel` model of the wanted pixel type (random unlocked, floor-lying type) floating in its centre (`AnimateModels`; the other holes show nothing). A husk whose middle comes inside `captureFraction` (0.85) of a hole's radius falls in (`TakePixels`: `ReleaseOldPixel` + `OldPixelDespawn.Begin`, which makes it a trigger so it tumbles down through the floor while shrinking): the WANTED type in the CORRECT hole pays its normal value (`ReleaseOldPixel(body, true)`) and has `ultraChance` (10%) of an Ultra pixel; anything else is simply taken away (no payout, no penalty). **The marker jumps to another hole after every correct drop and after `swapIdleSeconds` (12) without one** (`NextHole`; `changeTypeOnSwap` also changes the wanted type, off by default). A very gentle pull acts only in a thin band outside each hole's edge (`pullAcceleration` 5, `pullBandFraction` 0.5; nothing drawn). An event-log line sums up a round. Sounds `pad_spawn/_click/_wrong/_ultra`. **Ultra pixels are ONE general currency** (`PixelClicker.TotalUltra` held / `UltraEarned` lifetime, `AddUltra(amount)`, `RollUltra(chance)`, `RestoreUltra`; saved as `SaveData.ultraPixels`, older saves add up the old per-pixel `TierSave.ultraCount`; shown as an animated row at the very top of the Inventory's Currency tab with a line under it - `PixelUltraSwatch`: rainbow-cycling chip, glow, shine sweep, sparkles) for future upgrades; the minigame's tracker shows Ultra pixels ever earned (`PixelClicker.UltraEarned`, saved via the tracker count; spending Ultra on boosts does not lower it).  **Ultra is now a general minigame reward:** `PixelClicker.RollUltra(chance)` + `PixelMinigame.UltraRoll(clicker, chance, ...)` (per-round cap `ultraRoundCount`, event-log line, and `PixelUltraFx.Play` at the pixel's position: a rising gold "+1 Ultra Pixel" text, a sparkle burst and the sound `ultra_gain`; the Ultra Pad keeps its own text/sound and only adds the sparkles) are called by Snake (each eaten pixel of a won round + a guaranteed one), Sorting Race (each pixel of a win, chance x (1 + time left fraction)), Breakout (each broken brick) and Black Hole (each swallowed pixel); each has `ultraChance`, `ultraMaxPerRound`, `disableUltra` in the Inspector (0 = the coded default 4% / 6% / 3% / 1%, caps 3 / 3 / 3 / 2). Extends `PixelMinigame` (id `pad`); sounds `pad_spawn/_click/_wrong/_ultra`.
