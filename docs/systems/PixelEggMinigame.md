## PixelEggMinigame.cs / PixelBeanMinigame.cs

**Two very rare cat visitors** (ids `egg`, `bean`; the FIRST visit ever (`FirstVisitNow`, counter `<id>.met`, `TracksMeeting`) gives the special gift below, later visits bring a random present of pixels (`GivePixelGift`: `giftTypes` 3 random unlocked types, each worth `giftClicksMin..Max` (30-120) normal clicks of that pixel); started by `PixelGuideVendor.StartVendors` at 4 / 6 unlocked pixel types, first slot 10-25 min later; `PixelVisitorMinigame` got the hooks `IsGift` (accepting runs `OnGift()` after the optional second speech box instead of opening a wares window), `TracksMeeting` (the first real visit sets `<id>.met`; dev Spawn doesn't count), `VisitChance` (rolled once each time the visit timer runs out; a failed roll waits for the next timer) and `CanVisit`, plus `AcceptLabel` / `RejectLabel`). **Egg** = an orange cat; gives an egg (`PixelEgg`: a white cube with rounded corners built by `PixelEgg.RoundedCubeMesh`, stands in for the cube (it takes the cube's place and size, the cube shrinks away via `SetCubeShrink` so the auto clicker pauses, and returns when the egg hatches), clicked via a screen-distance test, `PixelPointerClaim` blocks cube clicks; 1-50 clicks rolled when given (`clicksMin/Max`), thin dark crack slivers appear as it takes hits; the last click starts the **hatching show** (`PixelEggHatch.cs`, component added by `PixelClicker.Awake`, `disableShow`; the egg hides and stays until it ends): a dark canvas (691) over the screen, the egg and pet drawn by a hidden studio camera into a full-canvas RenderTexture (1 studio unit = 100 canvas units; egg = a shell + a detachable lid of rounded cubes with glowing crack slivers): zoom to the middle, shake harder (`shakeSeconds`) with chips and cracks lighting up, a flash as the lid flies off, the pet peeks out of the shell, hops out and jumps about with sparkles (`jumpSeconds`), then hops into the backpack icon like a Dragon Cube; Escape skips (`PixelWindows` 165). The pet is `PixelPets.Award`ed when it ends (`FinishHatch`, quietly - the show replaces the found popup; if the show can't play the old shell burst + popup are used) (`PetCandidates`: not rare drops / standalone); state in counters `egg.pending/need/clicks`, restored after a load). **Bean** = a grey tabby cat; gives **Magic Beans** (a seed-kind device with `Device.beans`, craft-only, hold 1, `PixelConsumables.BeansItem()`; planted like seeds from the Inventory's Seeds category: `PlantSeed` -> `PixelBeanstalk.Plant`, needs a Dragon Cube you don't hold, `PickMissingDragonCube`): `PixelBeanstalk` grows a twisting stalk over 7 s with leaves and 5-7 random pixel cubes hanging on stems, one of them the Dragon Cube; click a cube to pick it (ordinary = a paid old pixel at 3x, Dragon Cube = `AddCurrency` + `PixelDragonFind.Play`, `PixelPointerClaim` blocks cube clicks); left alone it withers after 15 min and still hands over the Dragon Cube; the pending cube is kept in `bean.pending` (tier + 1) and the stalk regrows after a load.
