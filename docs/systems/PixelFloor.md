# PixelFloor

Moved from CLAUDE.md. The script header comments and `CLAUDE.md` index point here.

> **LATER CHANGES:** Spiral Galaxy and Black Hole were REMOVED (enum values `Galaxy` / `BlackHole` stay for saves; `defaultsVersion` 10). Radar has no circles any more (no range rings, no round dimming, no ping rings; the blips are spread over the whole screen). Liquid Flow (cells 12, detail 3), Circuit Flow (cells 12, thicker traces), Cyber Hex (cells 13) and Watching Eyes (cells 11) are zoomed in (bigger features).

> **UPDATE (big floors, 2026-10):** the 8-bit floors (Platformer, Invaders, Brick Breaker, Pong, Snake, Block Stack, Dungeon, Crossing, Road Racer, Fighter) were REMOVED (their enum values stay so saves keep working; `defaultsVersion` 9 drops them from saved lists). **Big non-repeating floors** (`PixelFloorLayers.cs`): Marble, Lava, Deep Space, Ice, Matrix Rain, Radar, Battle Crater, the four Auras, Death Energy, Liquid Flow, Spiral Galaxy, Black Hole, Moon Surface, Red Planet, Circuit Flow, Cyber Hex, Haunted Tiles, Graveyard and Watching Eyes are now ONE huge picture (about 36-40 world units, 512-1024 px) instead of a small repeating tile: `FloorStyle.detail` multiplies the noise lattices so features keep their size (`shadeDetail`), static ones use `stretchToFloor` (one copy, no repeat), scrolling ones use a huge tile (Lava, Space, Matrix, auras, wisps, Liquid). Big textures are drawn a few ms per frame (`FillSliced`, `UsesSlicedFill`; a soft blurred version of the picture (`FillPreview`, sampled on a 96 x 96 grid) shows until the sharp one is done, `PixelFloor.Drawing`). Menu icons are built small (`PreviewTexture` clones the style at <= 160 px). **Overlay styles** (`IsOverlayPattern`: Radar, Circuit Flow, Watching Eyes): the still picture is drawn once (`shadeBase`), then `OverlayFrame` repaints only what moves (radar blips + pings, circuit sparks, blinking eyes) into a cached Color32 copy - far cheaper than redrawing the whole texture. **Craters** (`ShadeCraterField`, `PrepareCraters`): one big crater where the camera looks (`SetCraterFocus`), several medium ones (`cells`) and many pits, lit from the upper left; `variant` 0 battle (scorch + glowing cracks), 1 moon (rays), 2 red planet. Haunted Tiles flickers by dimming the material (`UpdateHauntedFlicker`). Spiral Galaxy / Black Hole are drawn once at 1024 px (`ShadeGalaxyHQ`, `ShadeBlackHoleHQ`) and only pulse. Laser Corridor is scaled up (tile 18). Rain (`PixelSkyboxRain.cs`) is now one big sheet in front of the camera so it covers the whole view. **Animation cost:** animated floors are never redrawn faster than `animFpsCap` (0 = 10 per second) and not at all while the floor is invisible (faded out on the menu / 'Display floor' off; `unseen` in `Update`). Anything below that still mentions 8-bit floors or the old small tiles is out of date.

**Game-made floor (added later):** `PixelFloor.InitFloor` (Start, then once a second until it works) builds `Pixel Ground` with `BuildOwnFloor`: a flat code-made quad (`ownFloorSize` 0 = 80 world units, UVs 0..1 like a Unity plane) with a thin BoxCollider whose top is `groundFallDistance` (0 = 10 world units) under the cube's resting spot - the old pixels' falling distance. The scene's floor object is found (`FindFloor`, which ignores dev shapes) only to be hidden, its collider switched off and its collider material copied (else friction 0.6); tick `useSceneFloor` to keep using it (and the game still builds its own when the scene has none). Every floor style works on the game-made floor exactly as on a scene floor. **'Display floor' defaults to OFF** (only the sky shows, the collider still catches pixels) and `PixelFloorModes.cs` adds 'When floor is off' (Settings > Display, `PixelFloor.OffMode`): Invisible / Flat plane (an opaque lit sheet in the style's average colour, rimmed by the edge fade) / Landing pulse (`PixelFloorPulse.Landed` from `OldPixelImpact`: a rounded-square ripple on the surface the pixel hit, turned to the contact normal).

**World-space ground shader (first slice):** `Assets/Resources/PixelWorldGround.shader` (unlit CG, alpha, works in the built-in pipeline and URP; loaded with `Resources.Load` so builds include it) draws Lava / Marble / Ice / Deep Space from the WORLD position per pixel (no tiling, no stretching, sharp at any zoom / angle, `_Time` animation, faded round `_Center` between `_FadeStart` and `_FadeEnd`). A style opts in with `FloorStyle.shaderGround` (1 lava, 2 marble, 3 ice, 4 space; 0 = painted texture) and `shaderScale` (world units per pattern cell, 0 = 6); `PixelFloor.ApplyShaderGround` (PixelFloorModes.cs) builds the material (colours A/B/C, glow, seed) and falls back to the painted texture if the shader is missing; the menus' preview / title pictures stay CPU-painted, the edge-fade ring is off for shader grounds (the shader fades itself, `UpdateGroundShader`), `ApplyVisibility` just sets `_Fade`. **The game-made ground is lifted until it is on screen** (`UpdateGroundHeight`, every 0.5 s, normal view via `PixelOrbitCamera.HomeCamera`; `allowGroundOffScreen` turns it off): below the screen's bottom edge `PixelViewBounds`' bottom plane acts as a false floor and pixels never touch the real ground (no landing pulse, odd falling in the flat view). `OldPixelImpact` also fires the landing pulse for soft landings (>= 0.6 speed, own 0.08 s gap).

**Edge fade update:** `FadeRadii` (PixelFloorFade.cs) gives the fade's start / end radii to the ring, the wireframe AND the ground shader (one rim, smoother-step alpha). A painted one-copy picture (`stretchToFloor`) is faded out before its own edge (`tileWorldSize * 0.5 * 0.94`), so its clamped edge pixels never show smeared outwards. Shader grounds keep the digital wireframe rim (their own alpha does the fade, the opaque ring is hidden).


**Fourth batch - ten big world floors (`PixelFloorWorlds.cs`, `partial class PixelFloor`, dispatched from `ShadeRetro`'s fall-through to `ShadeWorlds`; styles from `WorldStyles()`):** Lush Grass, Nebula Field, Still Pond, Graffiti Wall, Leaf Litter, Zen Garden, Persian Rug, Coral Reef, Casino Felt, Stained Glass. Template = the big still floors: 1024 px, `stretchToFloor`, `tileWorldSize` 40, drawn once (time-sliced fill), no animation. Unlike older big floors they draw in WORLD units (`Wu(s)` = tileWorldSize, `Per(s, feature)` = noise lattice period for a feature of that many world units, hash grids in world units), so features keep their size at any resolution and in the small menu previews. New enum values `LushGrass` ... `StainedGlass` are appended after `Crossing`; saved lists pick the new styles up by name (`EnsureDefaultStyles`). The Graffiti Wall's bubble-letter tag (`PIXEL`) is drawn from stroke segments (`TagLetters`, `TagDistance`): outline, fill, highlight, drop shadow, overspray, drips.
