# PixelFloor

Moved from CLAUDE.md. The script header comments and `CLAUDE.md` index point here.

> **LATER CHANGES:** Spiral Galaxy and Black Hole were REMOVED (enum values `Galaxy` / `BlackHole` stay for saves; `defaultsVersion` 10). Radar has no circles any more (no range rings, no round dimming, no ping rings; the blips are spread over the whole screen). Liquid Flow (cells 12, detail 3), Circuit Flow (cells 12, thicker traces), Cyber Hex (cells 13) and Watching Eyes (cells 11) are zoomed in (bigger features).

> **UPDATE (big floors, 2026-10):** the 8-bit floors (Platformer, Invaders, Brick Breaker, Pong, Snake, Block Stack, Dungeon, Crossing, Road Racer, Fighter) were REMOVED (their enum values stay so saves keep working; `defaultsVersion` 9 drops them from saved lists). **Big non-repeating floors** (`PixelFloorLayers.cs`): Marble, Lava, Deep Space, Ice, Matrix Rain, Radar, Battle Crater, the four Auras, Death Energy, Liquid Flow, Spiral Galaxy, Black Hole, Moon Surface, Red Planet, Circuit Flow, Cyber Hex, Haunted Tiles, Graveyard and Watching Eyes are now ONE huge picture (about 36-40 world units, 512-1024 px) instead of a small repeating tile: `FloorStyle.detail` multiplies the noise lattices so features keep their size (`shadeDetail`), static ones use `stretchToFloor` (one copy, no repeat), scrolling ones use a huge tile (Lava, Space, Matrix, auras, wisps, Liquid). Big textures are drawn a few ms per frame (`FillSliced`, `UsesSlicedFill`; a soft blurred version of the picture (`FillPreview`, sampled on a 96 x 96 grid) shows until the sharp one is done, `PixelFloor.Drawing`). Menu icons are built small (`PreviewTexture` clones the style at <= 160 px). **Overlay styles** (`IsOverlayPattern`: Radar, Circuit Flow, Watching Eyes): the still picture is drawn once (`shadeBase`), then `OverlayFrame` repaints only what moves (radar blips + pings, circuit sparks, blinking eyes) into a cached Color32 copy - far cheaper than redrawing the whole texture. **Craters** (`ShadeCraterField`, `PrepareCraters`): one big crater where the camera looks (`SetCraterFocus`), several medium ones (`cells`) and many pits, lit from the upper left; `variant` 0 battle (scorch + glowing cracks), 1 moon (rays), 2 red planet. Haunted Tiles flickers by dimming the material (`UpdateHauntedFlicker`). Spiral Galaxy / Black Hole are drawn once at 1024 px (`ShadeGalaxyHQ`, `ShadeBlackHoleHQ`) and only pulse. Laser Corridor is scaled up (tile 18). Rain (`PixelSkyboxRain.cs`) is now one big sheet in front of the camera so it covers the whole view. **Animation cost:** animated floors are never redrawn faster than `animFpsCap` (0 = 10 per second) and not at all while the floor is invisible (faded out on the menu / 'Display floor' off; `unseen` in `Update`). Anything below that still mentions 8-bit floors or the old small tiles is out of date.

Picked in the pause menu's Settings (Display section, "Floor style" row) or the small picker right of the title screen's Play button (`PixelFloor.Instance.Step/SetStyle`, `PreviewTexture`; implements `IPixelLookSource`). Animations use unscaled time (they move on the title screen) and freeze during Time Stop, saved in PlayerPrefs `PixelClicker.Setting.FloorStyle`.


**Game-made floor (added later):** `PixelFloor.InitFloor` (Start, then once a second while nothing is found) looks for the scene's floor; if none is found after 3 tries - or the `ignoreSceneFloor` tick box is on, which hides the scene floor and switches its collider off - `BuildOwnFloor` makes `Pixel Ground`: a flat code-made quad (`ownFloorSize` 0 = 80 world units, UVs 0..1 like a Unity plane) with a thin BoxCollider whose top is the scene floor's top (or `ownFloorDrop` 0 = 1 pixel widths below the cube's resting spot) and the scene floor's collider material (else friction 0.6). Every floor style works on it exactly as on a scene floor. **'Display floor' now defaults to OFF**: the floor is invisible (only the sky shows) until the player ticks it, but its collider always catches pixels.
