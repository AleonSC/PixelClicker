## PixelSorterDevice.cs

(extends PixelPlacedDevice) Placed Sorter (device `kind` = Sorter): a ring (runtime-built mesh) around the cube facing the camera, a **bendable pipe** (constant-curvature tube mesh rebuilt by `Parts.Apply`) and **three force buttons** on the ring (click them; `sorterForceMultipliers`, selected one is brighter; screen-space hit test in `OnTick`; sound `sorter_button`). Placing = right-click in inventory; step 1 preview ring + straight pipe, scroll / Q / E turns it, left-click confirms; step 2 a cone (`sorterBendConeDegrees`) shows, the pipe end follows the mouse inside it (`TryGetBend`), left-click inside the cone places, right-click goes back to step 1 (`PixelConsumables.UpdateSorterPlacement`). While working, `PixelClicker.SpawnFallingCopy` asks static `PixelSorterDevice.Current.TryRoute` and spawns each old pixel at the pipe mouth with a velocity along the exit heading x force (slight wobble) instead of the random pop (not for fly-away tiers). **Streamed pixels have no collision in the air** (`OldPixelStream`, PixelStream.cs: the collider is a trigger, so the pixels of a stream don't knock each other / the ring / the cube about; each raycasts down, and on reaching the floor is placed on it, nudged away from pixels already lying there, ignores collisions with what it still overlaps, and only then becomes solid; its landing triggers Water splashes / Glass shatters via `OldPixelSplash.Land` / `OldPixelShatter.StreamLand`; Bank-spat pixels are unchanged). Settings are in the `Device` class ("Sorter only" fields); default sorter is auto-added like the fan. **Adjustable after switching on:** drag the pipe's END to bend it (relative drag, within `sorterBendConeDegrees`), drag the RING to turn the pipe round the cube (`Drag.Bend` / `Drag.Aim`, `FindHover`, `UpdateDrag`), and drag the **force slider** - a thin track beside the pipe with a knob and an 'x1.35' label (`Parts.UpdateSlider` / `ProjectSlider`) - to set ANY force between `sorterSliderMin` (0 = 0.2) and `sorterSliderMax` (0 = 3); the three buttons still jump to their presets (`forceValue`, `SetForceValue`, saved as `PlacedState.forceValue`). While the mouse is over any handle `PixelSorterDevice.HandleHot` blocks cube clicks.
