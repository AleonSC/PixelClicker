Device Map

Every husk machine in Pixel Clicker and how husks and power travel between them. Click a device to read its spec sheet and light up what it connects to.

Built from the scripts' header notes and docs/systems/ConnectionMap.md on 2026-10-23. Husks only move between devices through couplings (belts, hoses and fitted ports); power is a separate radius mechanic. Belt add-ons ride on a belt rather than coupling to it.
