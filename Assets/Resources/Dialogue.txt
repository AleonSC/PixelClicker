[Intro tips]
log: This is your Log. It tracks your achievements and your goals - what to work towards next. (Your click counts per pixel are in Settings > Stats.) Open it any time with the Log button at the bottom left.
inventory: This is your Inventory. Currency shows how many of each pixel you hold - you spend them in the shop. Consumables holds your potions and devices. Open it any time with the Inventory button at the top left.
earn: Clicking the pixel is the main way to earn currency. As you play, upgrades, minigames and managing the husks that fall to the floor will open up other ways to earn.
right click: Try the right mouse button too: HOLD it on the block on the cube. It's each block's second action. On the pure pixels (black, gray, white, red, green and blue) it puts a stake on the block - the longer you hold (watch the meter by your cursor), the more it is worth when it comes round again, but the stake can also go bad and pay nothing. Skill blocks you meet later do their own thing when held.

[Tutorial: Shop]
step 1: Welcome to the Shop! The list on the left shows every pixel you hold - prices are paid in those pixels. The tabs along the top split up what is for sale.
step 2: Pixels: packs that unlock new pixel types. Every new type is its own currency, and some need goals from the minigames before you can buy them.
step 3: Upgrades - Features: one-time unlocks such as the Auto Clicker, Pixel Grabbing, Time Stop and the Pixel Bank, plus upgrades for them.
step 4: Upgrades - Value: makes a pixel type pay more, paid in that same pixel. Each level costs a little more than the last. Shift-click buys as many levels as you can afford.
step 5: Upgrades - Operation: improves your consumables. Each one has a spinning picture and an orange arrow that opens its upgrades: longer lasting, bigger reach, more uses, more storage.
step 6: Consumables: things with a one-time use - potions, devices, seeds and supplies. Buy them here, then use them from the Consumables tab of your Inventory.
step 7: Minigames: events that start on their own once you buy them, from ghosts and meteors to Snake and Breakout. They pay rewards and unlock rare pixels. Every minigame can be switched on or off in the Toggles window, so you can customize your gameplay experience.
step 8: Anything greyed out shows what it still needs. Open the Shop again any time with the Shop button at the top right.

[Tutorial: Toggles]
step 1: Psst - you bought the Auto Clicker! It starts switched OFF. See the Toggles tab on the right edge of the screen? Every on / off switch in the game lives there. Let me open it.
step 2: Upgrades: here is your Auto Clicker. Tick it to start it, untick it to stop. Pixel Grabbing and Time Stop will show up here as soon as you own them.
step 3: Only things you own are listed. Pixels: some special pixels (like Glass) spawn on their own, so switch off the ones you don't want. Minigames, Vendors and Pets get their own groups, so you can shape your own game.

[Tutorial: Skills]
step 1: Welcome to Skills! Every pixel you collect now trains a skill. Standard levels from the very first click, and the skill packs you buy add skills of their own.
step 2: The bar along the top shows the skill you are training, its level and its XP. Fill it and the skill levels up.
step 3: Pick a skill on the left. Standard is always there, every pack you unlock adds a card, and locked ones show ???. The orange ! on a card says what it swaps out and what it spawns.
step 4: Press Train to put a skill in play. A pack swaps the basic cubes in your rotation for its own pixels, and only that skill earns XP. XP is kept per skill, so you can switch back and forth whenever you like.
step 5: Each level unlocks a perk, listed on the right. Standard's perks work in every rotation, like longer-lasting husks and a roomier floor.
step 6: Every level above 1 also gives a skill point. Spend it in the Skill tree: open it with the Skill tree button on a skill's page.
step 7: Careful: skill packs bring bad blocks too. Right-click a hazard to defuse it safely, or it goes off and costs you XP. What you do with husks matters as well: clearing out the useful ones earns bonus XP, junk costs it.
step 8: The Mastery bar along the bottom adds up all your skills. It only fills completely when every skill is maxed out.
step 9: The pin button turns your current skill into a small window with an XP bar you can move around. Open Skills any time with the tab on the left edge of the screen.

[Guide: Woodcutting]
question: First time chopping? Want a quick tutorial on how Woodcutting works?
later: No problem! Whenever you want it, the <b>guide book</b> next to the skill's name opens the Woodcutting guide.
step 1: <b>Woodcutting</b> is a skill. Press <b>Train</b> and the cube stops showing pure pixels and grows a forest instead. Every woodcutting block you break pays its own currency and gives Woodcutting XP.
step 2: Between trees the cube shows <b>filler</b>: Twig, Leaf and Wood at random (Moss and Sap join at level 2). Filler is quick and cheap - and the higher your level, the shorter the wait for the next tree.
step 3: Then a <b>tree</b> arrives and plays out <b>in order</b>: leaves first, then a run of Wood, and its special blocks near the end - Sap (level 2+), Acorn (level 3+) and Heartwood (level 6+). The <b>tree bar</b> at the top shows how far along you are.
step 4: Break a tree's last block for <b>Timber!</b> - bonus XP for the whole tree. Once you know a tree's shape you know what's coming next.
step 5: From level 2 some trees are <b>infested</b>: the tree bar warns you, and the tree ends in a <b>Termite Hive</b>, a tough hazard block. Pesticide (it shows up in a tree's trunk now and then) and the Lumberjack's Focus potion help.
step 6: Rare: the <b>Great Oak</b>. From level 3 a tree can be a Great Oak - one giant block that shakes bird nests loose (click them for XP). <b>Acorn</b> husks left lying on the field make it more likely.
step 7: Every level gives a <b>skill point</b> for the <b>Skill tree</b>, and Big Tim the <b>Lumberjack</b> visits while you chop - he buys your wood for pure pixels. Open this guide again any time with the <b>book</b> next to the skill's name.
