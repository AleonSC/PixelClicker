// Lines starting with // are notes for you and are not shown.
// A line starting with # is a heading. A line like  Key | What it does  is shown as two columns.
// Keep the right-hand part short so it stays on one line.
// {key:TimeStop}, {key:TimeSlow}, {key:Hose}, {key:EventLog}, {key:RotateLeft}, {key:RotateRight}, {key:ViewWindow} are replaced by the player's current key bindings (Settings > Key Bindings).

# Mouse
Left-click the cube | Hit the block (collect it)
Hold right-click on the cube | The block's second action (Block Hold): a stake on pure pixels, the block's own job on skill blocks
Right-click a hazard block | Clear it safely (some need several clicks)
Right-click a husk | Crush it for skill XP (more in the air / its last seconds / a streak; never money)
Hold right-click on a husk | Charge Crush (Standard tree): let go to crush its type inside the ring
Tap right-click on a skill's filler | Shuffle it back (starts a shuffle combo)
Hover a block or husk | Show its tooltip
Mouse to a screen edge | Slide out the docked buttons
Hold right-click on a potion line | Cancel the running potion (no refund)

# Keyboard
Esc | Pause menu / close the top window
{key:ViewWindow} | Open / close the View window
{key:TimeStop} | Stop / resume time (Time Stop upgrade)
{key:TimeSlow} | Slow time / back to normal (Time Slow upgrade)
{key:Hose} | Equip / put away the Pixel Bank hose
{key:EventLog} | Show the whole event log (again to hide)
{key:Feedback} | Give feedback (text box + screenshots)
Alt (tap) | Cycle block tooltips: Full / Minimal / Off
Alt (hold) | Peek at the other tooltip kind
Space (Inventory pinned) | Flip between pixels and consumables

# Pixel Grabbing
Hold left-click on a husk | Pick it up and drag it
Let go | Drop it, or throw it if you are moving
Let go slowly | Set it down gently

# Block Hold
Hold right-click on the cube | Hold the block while the meter drains
Let go | The block goes back, next in line
Hold a pure pixel (white, gray, black, red, green, blue) | Put a stake on it (green = pays more)
Keep clicking while holding | Raises the stake and refills the meter

# View and camera ({key:ViewWindow})
Ctrl + mouse wheel | Zoom (or flying speed in Free cam)
Middle mouse drag | Turn (and tilt in Orbital)
Arrow keys | Turn / tilt in Orbital and Top down
View window tick boxes | First person / Orbital / Top down / Free cam
Orthographic tick box | Flat view (not in first person)
Free cam: W A S D, Space / C | Fly about, up / down
Free cam: Shift | Fly fast

# First person
W A S D / arrows | Walk
Shift | Run
Space | Jump
Mouse | Look around
Left-click the clicker cube | Click it
Left-click a husk | Pick it up
Mouse wheel (holding) | Closer / farther
Hold right-click + mouse (holding) | Turn the held cube
Tap left-click (holding) | Stick it on the white ghost, or set it down
Hold left-click, let go (holding) | Throw it
Left-click a build | Pick up the whole build
Hold right-click on a stuck cube | Pry it loose
Mouse wheel / 1-5 (empty hands) | Pick a hotbar slot (slot 1 = axe)
Esc | Leave first person

# Placing devices
Right-click an item in the Inventory | Drink a potion / start placing a device
Left-click the floor | Place the device
Mouse wheel or {key:RotateLeft} / {key:RotateRight} | Turn it while placing
Hold Shift | Free placement (no grid or turn steps)
Right-click | Go back / cancel
Left-click a placed device | Wake it up (its timer starts)
Hold right-click on a placed device | Remove it (no refund)

# Device controls
Drag a carry handle | Move a Storage Crate, Recycler or Bait Bucket
Mouse wheel / {key:RotateLeft} / {key:RotateRight} (dragging) | Turn it
Click near the cube's edge | Its husk flies out of that side (aim husks at the Recycler)
Recycler | Husks that land in it are recycled by themselves (the red button empties anything left inside)
Click the Storage Crate | Store the husks lying on it
Click a stored pixel | Take it back out
Sorter: left-click (step 1) | Confirm the pipe's direction
Sorter: left-click in the cone | Place it with that bend
Sorter buttons 1-3 | Change how hard it spits
Magnetized Sorter: click a pipe, then a spot | Move that exit pipe
Magnetized Sorter: wheel over a pipe | Choose what that pipe shoots
Conveyor Belt Kit: click, click | Lay a belt between two points
Conveyor Belt Kit: mouse wheel | Raise or lower the belt's end
Conveyor Belt Kit: right-click | End the chain

# Pixel Bank hose ({key:Hose})
Mouse wheel | Choose which stored pixel to use
Click a Bank row | Select that pixel type
Move the mouse | The nozzle follows; the red dot shows its aim
Left-click | Drop the selected pixel on the red dot
Right-click | Suck up the husk under the red dot
Hold right-click | Suck up an area of the selected type

# Minigames and visitors
Click the ghost or meteor | Get the reward
Click a wire (Bomb) | Cut it
Arrow keys or on-screen arrows | Steer the snake (Snake)
Mouse or left / right arrows | Move the paddle (Breakout)
Click two husks | Swap them (Sorting Race)
Click every glowing spot | Catch the fish (Weak Spots)
Click a peeking visitor | Start the visit
X on a visitor's bubble | Send them away

# Shop
Shift-click a Value upgrade | Buy as many levels as you can afford
Hold the blue ? button | Replay the shop tour

# Crafting
Click or drag an item | Put it in a box
Right-click a box | Empty it

# UFO Helper (ride along)
Mouse | Look around the cabin
W A S D / arrows | Walk about the cabin
Esc | Climb out

# Collection Log gallery
Mouse | Look around
W A S D / arrows | Walk
Shift | Run
Esc | Leave

# Moving popups and tips
Hold left-click on an achievement popup | Pick it up; click to pin it, right-click resets
Hold left-click on the Did you know box | Pick it up; click to drop or dock at an edge
Quick click on the Did you know box | Close the tip

# Saving
Hold right-click on a save slot | Delete that save

# Menu shortcuts
Settings > Change keybinds | Keys for Inventory, Shop, Log, Crafting, Toggles, Bank, Skills
Backspace (binding screen) | Clear a key
Pocket > Keybinds tab | Bind any potion, device or tool to your own key
