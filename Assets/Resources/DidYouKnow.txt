You can hold the left mouse button on this box to move it. Put it at a screen edge to dock it there.
Every pixel type is its own currency. The early colours are unlocked by the goals in your Log: crush husks for Gray, recycle them for Black.
The pixels that fall off the cube are husks. They are material, not money - find something useful to do with them before they fade.
Holding the right mouse button on the cube is each block's second action (Block Hold, from Standard's skill tree): a stake on pure pixels, a special job on skill blocks.
Holding a pure pixel (white, gray, black, red, green, blue) puts a stake on it: when it comes round again it pays more - or nothing.
Hover over the cube or a husk to see what it is. The Pixel Guide tells you more about every block.
Every husk that lands in the Recycler pays back half its value and some XP. Several of the same colour in a row build a streak for more XP.
Grab the handles on the Recycler or the Storage Crate to carry them somewhere else. Scroll while carrying to turn them.
In first person you can open the Recycler's back hatch and toss husks in by hand.
Press {key:ViewWindow} to open the View window: first person, orbital or top down, plus a zoom slider.
Ctrl + mouse wheel zooms the camera. The plain wheel never moves it.
Move the mouse to a screen edge and the docked menu buttons slide out.
Press {key:EventLog} to open the event log (the Log window's third tab). Click a line to read its tip again.
Clicks are limited to about 14 per second, so a steady rhythm beats frantic clicking.
The game keeps a history of your last autosaves. Look for the Autosaves button in the Save / Load window.
You can bind your own keys to potions, devices and menu buttons in Settings > Key Bindings.
Give feedback from the pause menu - you can even draw on the screenshots.
Things you can't buy yet show in grey, along with what they need.
shop: Shift-click a Value upgrade to buy as many levels as you can afford.
shop: A potion is priced to pay back about three times what it cost.
shop: Right-click a potion in your Inventory to drink it. Drinking another one before it ends keeps it going.
shop: Right-click a device in your Inventory to place it. A placed device sleeps until you left-click it once.
shop: Hold the right mouse button on a placed device to remove it. There is no refund, so place carefully.
shop: Upgrades > Operation in the Shop makes your devices run longer, reach further and hold more.
shop: Minigames you buy turn up on their own while you play.
Hold the left mouse button on a husk to pick it up, then let go to drop or throw it.
shop: The Fan blows husks along the floor - handy for pushing them into a Recycler or a black hole.
shop: The Auto Clicker keeps clicking even while the game is closed - you get a Welcome back payout.
shop: Skill packs are chosen in the Skills window. Every block you break also trains the Standard skill.
shop: Each skill pack has its own hazard block. Learn what it does before it catches you out.
shop: Your Value upgrades for a skill's blocks are capped by that skill's level. Train the skill to raise the cap.
Seven Dragon Cubes are hidden among the random pixels. Hold all seven at once and something special happens.
The rarest cubes show as ??? in the Collection Log until you find them.
The Collection Log has a gallery you can walk around in to see what you have collected.
You can change the floor and the sky in Settings. Some floors even react to your mouse.
Turn on Flat view in Settings > Display for an orthographic look.
Screen filters like CRT TV, VHS and Noir are in Settings > Display.
You can turn these tips off in Settings > Gameplay.
