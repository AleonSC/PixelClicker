# The Goal
Click the cube to collect pixels. Every pixel type is its own currency. Collect enough of one type and the next, better type unlocks. Spend your pixels in the Shop to unlock new pixel types, upgrades, potions, devices and minigames.

# Collecting
Each click pays out the pixel the cube is showing. A smaller copy of that pixel then falls to the floor: an "old pixel". Old pixels vanish after a few seconds, but a lot of the game is about doing something useful with them before they do.
The cube is also clicked for you by the Auto Clicker once you buy it and switch it on (it starts off: open the Toggles window on the right edge, go to Upgrades and tick it), even while the game is closed (you get a "Welcome back" payout for the time away).
Clicks are limited to about 14 per second.

# The Shop
The Shop button appears once you have unlocked the Black pixel. It has four tabs:
<b>Pixels</b> - packs that unlock new pixel types (RGB, Glass, Vacuum, Obsidian and more).
<b>Upgrades</b> - the Auto Clicker and its upgrades, Combo Meter, Crafting, Pixel Grabbing, Time Stop and its Time Slow upgrade, the Pixel Bank and its storage upgrades. The "Value" sub-tab makes each pixel type pay more, paid for with that same pixel; every level costs a bit more than the last, and every 25 levels the payout doubles. Shift-click buys as many levels as you can afford. The "Features" sub-tab holds the feature unlocks. Ultra pixels are a rare second currency (hover a pixel in your Inventory to see its Ultra count); Snake, Breakout, Sorting Race and Black Holes can reward them too.
<b>Consumables</b> - potions and utilities (see below).
<b>Minigames</b> - events that appear on their own once bought.
Things you can't buy yet show in grey with what they need.

# Special Pixels
<b>Glass</b> - the ingredient for potions in Crafting.
<b>Vacuum</b> - when you collect one, every old pixel on the floor flies into the cube and pays out again.
<b>Obsidian</b> and <b>Luminescent</b> - tougher or rarer pixels worth more.
<b>Singularity</b>, <b>Ghost</b> and <b>Meteor</b> - rare pixels you earn by playing their minigames.
<b>Electric</b> - a cube of crackling lightning that pays a random amount each time. Clicking one overcharges your auto clicker with a burst of clicks, and Electric pixels lying on the floor arc to your placed devices: every pixel that links adds a second to the device, and they all vanish when it ends.

# The Dragon Cubes
Seven glassy orange cubes, each with 1 to 7 small dark cubes inside, turn up among the random pixels - extremely rarely, with no requirements. Click one to collect it. Hold all seven at once and a Summon the Dragon button appears: the cubes are used up, time stops, and a cube dragon offers you three wishes. Pick one: a big payout, a Golden Hour of triple income, Ultra pixels, potions, or a new pet.

# Potions and Devices
Open the inventory's Consumables tab (it has Potions and Devices sub-tabs). Right-click a potion to drink it: for a while only that pixel type spawns. Drink them one after another to keep it going. You can hold up to 10 of each potion. A potion is priced to pay back about 3 times what it cost, so its price grows with your clicking speed and that pixel's upgrades; crafting a potion costs a bit less in its pixel, plus the Glass. Device prices rise with the Value level of the pixels they cost. Three special items sit in the Devices list: Combo Fuel (hold 3) is used by itself when your combo bar runs out - it keeps the combo and refills the bar; Ghost Bait (hold 1, right-click) calls three slow ghosts sooner; Pet Treat (hold 5, right-click) makes your pets hyper and glowing for a minute and doubles the extra spawn chance they give. During a Vacuum potion only every 10th Vacuum pixel vacuums the floor.
Right-click a device (Vacuum Device, Fan, Sorter) to place it. The Vacuum Device pulls in old pixels and collects them again. The Fan blows old pixels along the floor. The Sorter wraps around the cube and spits every pixel you collect out of an aimable pipe.
Hold the right mouse button on a placed device to remove it, or on the "Active:" line at the top to cancel a potion. There is no refund.

# Minigames
<b>Ghost Hunt</b> - a faint cube floats by. Click it for a free potion. With Pixel Grabbing you can also catch the falling potion and drop it on the backpack icon to keep it - kept potions can take you past the usual potion limit.
<b>Black Hole</b> - a swirling hole opens on the floor and swallows old pixels. While it is open, old pixels stop vanishing. A counter above it shows how many pixels you have fed it out of the 250 needed to unlock the Singularity Pixel. Push old pixels toward it with the fan, Pixel Grabbing or the hose.
<b>Bomb Defusal</b> - a bomb appears. Cut the wire of the colour you own the most of (Red, Green or Blue) before the timer runs out. A wrong wire or running out of time clears your old pixels. Defusing earns bomb parts.
<b>Ultra Pad</b> - a coloured pad appears on one side of the cube. Throw or drop old pixels of that colour onto it for a chance at an Ultra version (the other minigames give a smaller chance for the pixels they use). Pixels of any other colour cost you some of that currency.
<b>Meteor Strike</b> - a meteor drifts by. Click it for meteor chunks.
<b>Sorting Race</b> - now and then some old pixels freeze in a jumbled grid. Click one pixel and then another to swap them, and get every type together in one unbroken group before the timer ends. The faster you finish, the bigger the payout; if time runs out they just drop.
<b>Snake</b> - now and then every old pixel freezes and stops despawning. Steer the snake head with the arrow keys (or the on-screen arrows): each pixel it touches joins its tail. Eat them all without biting yourself for 10x their worth; bite yourself or run out of time and they just drop.
Switch any of them off in the Toggles window.

# Upgrades Worth Knowing
<b>Pixel Grabbing</b> - click and drag old pixels, then let go to throw them.
<b>Time Stop</b> - press T to freeze everything, including timers. It drains an energy meter (shown only while it is in use); time resumes when you press T again or the meter runs out, and the meter refills right away. You can still click the cube while time is stopped - the old pixels pile up and burst out when time resumes. The Time Slow upgrade lets you slow time instead (press S): it drains the same meter more slowly, and meteor pixels crawl enough to be grabbed with Pixel Grabbing.
<b>Combo Meter</b> - quick clicks build a combo. Reaching a combo milestone (10, 50, 200, 500, 1000) raises your click bonus (x1.5 up to x5); each upgrade level unlocks the next milestone, and every milestone makes the meter flashier (shake, flames, glow, lightning, and finally an explosion).
<b>Crafting</b> - combine two items to make potions and more.
<b>Pixel Bank</b> - store old pixels and spit them back out. Press B for the hose. The nozzle normally points along the hose, but a quick flick of the mouse aims it the way you moved, so you can shoot in any direction. Click to suck up one pixel; hold the right button to suck up every old pixel of the selected type in a ring around the nozzle (with nothing selected, it takes the type of the pixel nearest the nozzle). Bank Storage upgrades raise how much it holds (they cost glass, vacuum pixels and bomb parts).

# Useful Windows
<b>Inventory</b> (top left) - your pixels, and your potions and devices.
<b>Log</b> (bottom left) - pixel totals and achievements.
<b>Toggles</b> (right edge) - switch pixel spawns, upgrades and minigames on or off.
<b>Bank</b> (left edge) - what the Pixel Bank holds.
Buttons slide out of the edge of the screen when your mouse gets close to them.

# Saving
The game saves by itself and when you quit. You can also Save and Load from the pause menu. Restart deletes everything.

# If Something Goes Wrong
The game writes error and crash reports to text files. If it crashes or misbehaves, open Pause > Report Folder and send the newest .txt file to the developer. If the game closed unexpectedly last time, a box tells you so when you start it.

# Pets
Very rarely, clicking a pixel gives you a pet: a pixel-sized cube of that type that roams the floor by itself, hopping and rolling around. You can only have one pet per pixel type, and each can be switched on or off in the Toggles window under Pets. Hover a pet to make it stand still, or click and drag it to pick it up and throw it. The Ghost pet floats instead of rolling. A pet that is switched on makes its pixel type spawn more often. Hovering a pet shows its name tag: click the pencil to rename it and change its outfit in a 3D view (drag to turn it, use the arrows to try outfits).
