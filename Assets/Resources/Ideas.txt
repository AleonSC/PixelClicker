# Device connections beyond husks (sockets + couplings)
- The socket / coupling system (belts and hoses snap to a coupling on a device socket) starts with HUSK flow; its socket kind is an open list so more connection kinds can be added later, each with its own coupling look.
- Power (Electrician): electricity that speeds machines up or keeps them running. Today Wire / Battery / Resistor husks and Electric pixels arc to devices in their own hand-made code; moving that onto power sockets gives Electrician a real job in the factory (e.g. a powered Recycler works faster). Plan this one next.
- Signal (the planned Automation skill): on / off triggers between devices - a full Storage Crate starts a Recycler, a hopper opens when a colour arrives. Turns a pile of machines into a factory. Plan this one next too.
- Water (Farming / Fishing): Sprinkler, Water pixels and the Bait Bucket's water piped between devices for growing and cooling.
- Heat (Firemaking, later Smithing): fuel and heat for the Kiln, Charcoal and burning.
- Link (late game): wireless pairs that pass a husk or effect between two devices, like the Quantum Organizer's teleport.

# More transports (later, after belt / tube / chute / robot arm / launcher)
- Water flume (Fishing / Farming): husks float along a water channel; floaty husks (Seaweed, Leaf) ride it best.
- Mag-rail (Electrician): husks hover along a powered rail - the fastest ground route.
- Teleport pads (late game, Quantum Organizer tech): a pair; husks entering one come out of the other, any distance.
- Drone courier (UFO Helper theme): flies batches between two pads every few seconds, over everything.
