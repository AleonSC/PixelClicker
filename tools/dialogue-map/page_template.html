How to use it

  1. Search or browse the sections below and click one.
  2. Its lines show on the right, drawn like the game's boxes, each with its ID and a copy button.
  3. Paste the ID into chat with what you want changed, or edit the line yourself in Assets/Resources/Dialogue.txt (the line number is shown).

Yellow {0}-style words are filled in by the game; blue <b> tags are formatting. Keep both when you reword.
