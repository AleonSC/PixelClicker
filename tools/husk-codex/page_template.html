Every block in Pixel Clicker: what a click on it does, what its husk does once it drops, and which other husks, sprouts and machines it touches.

Husks drop off the cube with the bounce, grip and gravity their look gives them in the game, plus their special behaviour: Leaf swings down, Ghost floats off after a few bounces, Glass shatters, Water splashes, Meteor streaks away, Fire burns out, Solar hovers, a Singularity pulls the others in, Nuclear makes its neighbours fade. Pick husks, then drop them. Click the floor to drop the picked ones there.
