Pixel Clicker Game Loop

How every part of the game feeds the others: click the cube, spend what it gives, grow, and come back stronger. Each station and arrow is coloured by how finished it is, so the unfulfilled parts of the loop stand out. Click any station for what it gives, what it needs and what is missing.
